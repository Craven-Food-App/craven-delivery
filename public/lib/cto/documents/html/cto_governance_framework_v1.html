1. Purpose Of This Framework

This Governance Framework defines how the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) establishes, operates, and maintains the technology governance environment for Crave'n. It provides enterprise-level guardrails that ensure engineering excellence, security, scalability, and alignment across all technology domains.

This framework is designed to mirror the standards used in Fortune 500 technology and engineering organizations, adapted to Crave'n's high-growth environment.

2. Governance Scope

The CTO's governance authority applies to the following domains:

  • Technology strategy, planning, and architecture
  • Platform infrastructure and operations
  • Engineering standards and code quality
  • Security, compliance, and data governance
  • System performance and reliability
  • Technology vendor and tool selection
  • Cross-functional technology alignment with COO, CXO, CFO, and CEO
  • Technology systems, tools, and internal technology data governance

3. Governance Principles

The CTO's governance system is built on five core principles.

3.1 Engineering Excellence

Technology systems must be reliable, scalable, secure, and maintainable. The CTO ensures the CEO always receives a clear and honest view of the company's technology position.

3.2 Security First

Security must be built into all technology decisions from the start. Security vulnerabilities and incidents are escalated immediately.

3.3 Scalability & Performance

The CTO defines infrastructure standards, performance requirements, and scalability guardrails. The CTO enforces these standards across all technology systems.

3.4 Alignment & Integration

All technology decisions must be aligned with operational and business realities. The CTO collaborates closely with COO, CXO, CFO, and CEO.

3.5 Innovation & Efficiency

Technology systems must enable innovation while maintaining cost efficiency and operational discipline.

4. Governance Operating Model

The CTO governance model consists of four structural layers.

4.1 Strategic Governance

  • Annual technology plan and roadmap
  • 3-year strategic technology vision
  • Technology investment and vendor strategy
  • Platform architecture and scalability governance

4.2 Operational Governance

  • Weekly, monthly, and quarterly technology reporting cadence
  • System performance monitoring and alerting
  • Infrastructure cost management and optimization
  • Approval workflows and delegation of authority for technology changes

4.3 Control Governance

  • Security controls and vulnerability management
  • System access governance and data integrity
  • Compliance monitoring and regulatory obligations
  • Risk identification, escalation, and mitigation

4.4 Information Governance

  • Ownership of technology systems and technology datasets
  • Engineering standards and code quality metrics
  • Data retention and audit readiness
  • Source-of-truth technology data architecture

5. Delegation Of Authority & Escalation

The CTO must operate within the enterprise Delegation of Authority (DOA) framework approved by the CEO. The CTO may further delegate operational-level responsibilities, but accountability remains with the CTO at all times.

5.1 Escalation Triggers

  • Material system downtime or performance degradation
  • Security breaches, vulnerabilities, or compliance risks
  • Non-compliance with security standards or regulatory requirements
  • Suspected technology failures or infrastructure risks
  • Any technology risk that may impact operations or business continuity

5.2 Escalation Path

  1. CTO → CEO (immediate escalation)
  2. CTO → COO/CFO/CXO (cross-functional impact)
  3. CTO → Board or Advisors (CEO direction required)

6. Governance Cadence

Governance is enforced through a disciplined operating cadence managed by the CTO.

6.1 Weekly

  • Technology snapshot to CEO
  • System performance and uptime update
  • Security alert review and response flags

6.2 Monthly

  • Full technology reporting packet
  • Variance analysis and performance narrative
  • Compliance and security checkpoint

6.3 Quarterly

  • Strategic technology review
  • Infrastructure cost and scalability review
  • Technology roadmap and vendor readiness update

7. Governance Outputs

The CTO produces structured governance artifacts that support execution and compliance.

  • Annual technology plan and roadmap
  • Weekly technology summary
  • Monthly reporting packet
  • Quarterly strategic review packet
  • Security and compliance schedule
  • Technology risk register
  • Delegation of authority and approval matrix

8. Governance Risk Domains

The CTO monitors risk across the following domains.

  • System uptime and performance
  • Security vulnerabilities and breaches
  • Infrastructure cost pressures
  • Technology vendor and tool risks
  • Compliance and regulatory risk
  • Engineering velocity and code quality

9. Amendment And Review

This Governance Framework will be reviewed annually by the CTO and CEO, or more frequently if required based on business scale or regulatory requirements.
