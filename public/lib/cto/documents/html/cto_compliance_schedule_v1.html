1. Purpose Of This Document

This Compliance & Regulatory Schedule outlines the entire security, technology compliance, and regulatory obligations that the Chief Technology Officer must maintain. This schedule follows Fortune 500 technology operations standards and ensures Crave'n remains compliant, audit-ready, and investor-ready at all times.

2. Core Compliance Domains

The CTO is responsible for compliance across these domains:

  • Security compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
  • Technology regulatory obligations
  • System access audit requirements
  • Security and vulnerability management
  • Data retention and deletion policies
  • Regulatory filings for technology systems
  • Technology vendor compliance
  • Internal controls and audit readiness for technology systems

3. Compliance Calendar Overview

This section defines all required compliance deliverables by frequency.

4. Monthly Compliance Obligations

  • Security compliance review
  • Vulnerability management checkpoint
  • Monthly system access audit
  • Monthly technology compliance & documentation archive
  • Technology vendor compliance updates
  • Compliance checklist confirmation to CEO

5. Quarterly Compliance Obligations

  • Security compliance review (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
  • Technology regulatory filings
  • Quarterly technology compliance review
  • Update technology risk register
  • Quarterly board-level technology packet (if applicable)

6. Annual Compliance Obligations

  • Annual security compliance certification
  • Annual technology compliance review
  • Annual report filing for technology systems (if applicable)
  • Year-end technology statements
  • Annual compliance certification to CEO
  • Year-end technology archive & system integrity audit

7. Event-Driven Compliance Obligations

  • Entering new states/markets with different security regulations
  • Signing material technology vendor agreements
  • Security breach incidents
  • Changes in security or technology regulations
  • Technology system launches requiring regulatory review

8. Documentation Retention Rules

  • Security compliance records: 7 years minimum
  • Technology compliance records: 7 years minimum
  • System access audit logs: 7 years minimum
  • Technology vendor agreements: Duration + 7 years
  • Security and technology compliance documents: Permanent

9. Escalation Requirements

The CTO must immediately escalate to the CEO if:

  • A compliance deadline will be missed
  • A security breach occurs
  • A compliance risk is identified
  • Regulatory correspondence is received

10. Compliance Reporting To CEO

A monthly compliance summary must be delivered that includes:

  • Upcoming deadlines
  • Completed obligations
  • Risks and flags
  • Escalations
  • Documentation status
