## Company Description

Crave'n is a next-generation food delivery company built on a simple principle: **when local businesses succeed, entire communities benefit**. We are redefining the delivery landscape by prioritizing fairness, transparency, and sustainable growth.

We were created with a mission to restore balance in an industry that often disadvantages small businesses. Our platform uses advanced logistics and modern technology to empower independent restaurants with the tools, insights, and operational support they need to compete at scale. We believe every neighborhood kitchen deserves the opportunity to thrive without sacrificing profits to excessive fees or restrictive practices.

Our commitment reaches far beyond convenience. We focus on:
- Strengthening merchant relationships
- Improving delivery infrastructure
- Creating meaningful earning opportunities for drivers
- Delivering an elevated customer experience centered on reliability, speed, accuracy, and trust

Crave'n is not just delivering meals. We are reshaping the future of local commerce by championing equitable economics, community-driven innovation, and uncompromising service quality.

Premium membership with unlimited perks and exclusive benefits.

#### Benefits

- ✅ Special member-only discounts
- ✅ Cancel anytime (no contracts)

### 3. Restaurant Partner Platform 🏪

Enterprise-grade commission management system designed to support restaurants at every stage of growth.

#### 5-Tier Performance System

| Tier | Icon | Monthly Volume | Commission | Key Benefits |
|------|------|----------------|------------|--------------|
| **Bronze** | 🥉 | $0 - $10k | 18% | Email support, Basic analytics |

| **Gold** | 🥇 | $50k - $100k | 12% | 24/7 support, Premium analytics, Homepage featured, Free monthly promo |
| **Platinum** | 💎 | $100k - $250k | 10% | Dedicated account manager, Custom reports, Priority placement, 2 free monthly promos |
| **Elite** | 👑 | $250k+ | 8% | Executive support, Real-time dashboard, Exclusive partnerships, Unlimited promos, Negotiable terms |

#### Key Features
- **Automatic Tier Upgrades** - Based on monthly order volume
- **Custom Commission Overrides** - For individual restaurant negotiations
- **Restaurant-Specific Pricing** - Flexible rate structures
- **Real-Time Analytics Dashboard** - Performance tracking and insights
- **Online Ordering** - Commission-free on restaurant's own website
- **6-Month Order Guarantee** - Refund if receiving fewer than 20 orders/month

#### Additional Restaurant Benefits
- Access to CraveMore members (30% increase in earnings typically)
- Professional restaurant dashboard
- Order management system

- Marketing support and promotions
