**Step 5: Document Upload**

**Step 6: Review & Submit**
- Review all information
- Accept Independent Contractor Agreement
- Accept Privacy Policy
- Submit application

#### 6.2.2 Waitlist System

**Purpose:** Collect verified driver applications before launching in a market, controlling costs by delaying background checks until routes are ready.

**How It Works:**
1. Driver completes full application (feels like immediate onboarding)
2. After submission, driver learns they're on waitlist
3. Status set to `waitlist` in database
4. Waitlist position calculated by city/state
5. Background check NOT initiated (saves $30-40 per driver)
6. Driver receives confirmation email with position number

**Waitlist Reveal Experience:**
- Full-screen success modal appears
- "You're on the List! 🎉" message
- Shows waitlist position (e.g., "#47 in Toledo")
- Estimated wait time (2-8 weeks)
- What happens next explanation
- Confirmation email sent automatically

**Activation Process:**
- Admin selects city/region for launch
- System suggests drivers from that area (sorted by position)
- Admin filters by vehicle type, application date, document status
- Admin sends activation invites
- Confirmation shows cost estimate (background checks)
- Activation email sent to selected drivers
- Background check initiated upon acceptance
- Driver completes onboarding tasks
- Driver activated and can start delivering

#### 6.2.3 Onboarding Tasks

Once activated, drivers must complete onboarding tasks:

1. **Complete Profile Setup** (25 points)
   - Fill out all required profile information
   - Verify details

2. **Upload Vehicle Photos** (30 points)
   - Take clear photos from multiple angles
   - Upload to system

3. **Pass Safety Quiz** (50 points)
   - Complete driver safety quiz
   - Passing score: 80% or higher

4. **Setup CashApp Payouts** (35 points)
   - Connect CashApp account
   - Enable instant payout processing

5. **Download Mobile App** (20 points)
   - Download Crave'n delivery mobile app
   - Log in successfully

6. **Complete First Practice Route** (25 points)
   - Complete practice delivery route
   - Familiarize with app interface

7. **Join Driver Facebook Group** (15 points)
   - Join exclusive driver community
   - Access tips and support

8. **Complete Customer Service Training** (30 points)
   - Watch training videos
   - Pass assessment

9. **Social Media Share** (10 points)
   - Share about joining Crave'n
   - Post on social media accounts

**Total Points Available:** 240 points

### 6.3 Feeder Requirements

#### Minimum Requirements
- **Age:** At least 18 years old
- **License:** Valid driver's license (if using vehicle)
- **Vehicle:** Car, bike, scooter, motorcycle, or ability to walk
- **Background Check:** Must pass background check (initiated upon activation)
- **Smartphone:** iOS or Android device with app capability
- **Banking:** Bank account or CashApp for payouts

#### Vehicle Requirements
- Valid registration
- Current insurance
- Vehicle in good working condition
- Clean vehicle (professional appearance)

### 6.4 Feeder Earnings

#### Earnings Structure

**Base Pay:**
- 70% of delivery fee charged to customer
- Example: $4.49 delivery fee = $3.14 base pay to driver

**Tips:**
- 100% of customer tips go to driver
- Tips are in addition to base pay
- Average tip: $3-7 per delivery

**Earnings Potential:**

| Time Period | Hours | Average Rate |
|-------------|-------|--------------|
| **Lunch Rush** | 11:30 AM - 1:30 PM | $18-25/hour |
| **Dinner Rush** | 5:30 PM - 8:30 PM | $20-28/hour |
| **Weekend Peak** | Fri-Sun Evenings | $22-30/hour |
| **Average (Peak Times)** | Varies | $15-25/hour |

**Factors Affecting Earnings:**
- Number of deliveries completed
- Tips received
- Peak hour multipliers
- Distance traveled
- Time of day/day of week

#### Payout Schedule

**Daily Automatic Payouts:**
- Earnings from previous day
- Processed automatically each morning
- Deposited via ACH (next-day) or instant methods
- No minimum threshold

**Instant Cashout:**
- Available for drivers with CashApp or instant payout methods
- Small fee may apply
- Immediate access to earnings

# Part III: Operations & Workflows

## 9. Ordering Process

### 9.1 Complete Order Journey

#### Step 1: Discovery & Selection
1. Customer opens Crave'n app or website
2. Enters delivery address or selects pickup
3. Browses available restaurants
4. Filters by:
   - Cuisine type
   - Delivery time
   - Price range
   - Dietary restrictions
   - Ratings
   - CraveMore benefits
5. Selects restaurant

#### Step 2: Menu Browsing
1. Views restaurant menu
2. Sees item details:
   - Name and description
   - Price
   - Images
   - Dietary information
   - Modifier options
3. Adds items to cart
4. Customizes items:
   - Modifiers (size, toppings, etc.)
   - Special instructions
   - Quantity

#### Step 3: Cart Review
1. Reviews cart contents
2. Sees order summary:
   - Itemized list
   - Subtotal
   - Service fee (if applicable)
   - Delivery fee (if applicable)
   - Estimated tax
   - Estimated tip
   - **Total**
3. Applies promo code (if available)
4. Adjusts quantities or removes items
5. Proceeds to checkout

#### Step 4: Checkout
1. **Delivery Method Selection:**
   - Delivery (to address)
   - Pickup (at restaurant)

2. **Address Information:**
   - Delivery address (saved or new)
   - Special delivery instructions
   - Leave at door option
   - Contact phone number

3. **Payment Method:**
   - Saved payment method
   - New card entry
   - ACH "Pay by Bank" option
   - Cash (pickup only)

4. **Tip Selection:**
   - Percentage options (15%, 18%, 20%, 25%)
   - Custom amount
   - No tip option

5. **Order Review:**
   - Final order summary
   - Total amount
   - Estimated delivery time
   - Restaurant contact info

6. **Place Order:**
   - Confirms order
   - Processes payment
   - Creates order record
   - Sends confirmation

#### Step 5: Order Confirmation
1. Order confirmation screen
2. Order number displayed
3. Estimated delivery time shown
4. Option to track order
5. Confirmation email/SMS sent

#### Step 6: Order Processing
1. **Restaurant Notification:**
   - Restaurant receives order
   - Accepts or rejects order
   - Begins preparation

2. **Driver Assignment:**
   - System assigns available driver
   - Driver accepts order
   - Driver notified of pickup location

3. **Preparation:**
   - Restaurant prepares order
   - Marks order as ready
   - Driver notified to pickup

#### Step 7: Pickup & Delivery
1. **Driver Pickup:**
   - Driver arrives at restaurant
   - Confirms pickup
   - Takes photo (optional)
   - Starts delivery

2. **In Transit:**
   - Real-time tracking active
   - Customer sees driver location
   - Estimated arrival time updates
   - Customer can contact driver

3. **Delivery:**
   - Driver arrives at delivery address
   - Completes delivery
   - Takes delivery photo (if leave at door)
   - Marks order as delivered

#### Step 8: Order Completion
1. **Delivery Confirmation:**
   - Customer receives notification
   - Order marked complete
   - Payment finalized

2. **Post-Delivery:**
   - Rating prompt (restaurant and driver)
   - Review option
   - Reorder option
   - Support access if needed

### 9.2 Order Types

#### Delivery Orders
- Delivered to customer's address
- Delivery fee applies (waived for CraveMore members)
- Real-time tracking available
- Driver assigned automatically
- Estimated delivery time: 30-60 minutes

#### Pickup Orders
- Customer picks up at restaurant

- **Base Pay:** 70% of delivery fee
- **Tips:** 100% of customer tips
- **Total:** Base pay + tips

#### Payout Schedule
- **Daily Automatic Payouts:**
  - Earnings from previous day
  - Processed each morning
  - ACH deposit (next-day)
  - No minimum threshold

- **Instant Cashout:**
  - Available via CashApp or instant methods
  - Small fee may apply
  - Immediate access

#### Payout Methods
1. **ACH (Standard):**
   - Next-day deposit
   - Bank account required
   - No fees

2. **Instant RTP:**
   - Same-day deposit
   - Real-time payment
   - Small fee

3. **CashApp:**
   - Instant transfer
   - Mobile app integration
   - Convenient

#### Payout Processing
1. Daily batch created
2. Earnings calculated
3. Payouts processed via Moov
4. Drivers notified
5. Funds deposited

### 17.2 Restaurant Payouts

#### Payout Calculation
```
Restaurant Payout = 
  Σ(Order Subtotals) 
  - Σ(Commissions) 
  - Σ(Adjustments: refunds, chargebacks)
  + Σ(Credits: promos, incentives)
```

#### Payout Schedule
- **Default:** Daily automated payouts
- **Frequency:** Every business day
- **Timing:** Morning processing
- **Method:** ACH to restaurant bank account

#### Payout Components
1. **Gross Sales:**
   - Sum of all order subtotals
   - Completed orders only

2. **Less Commission:**
   - Tier-based or override rate
   - Calculated per order

3. **Less Adjustments:**
   - Refund reversals
   - Chargeback deductions
   - Manual adjustments

4. **Plus Credits:**
   - Promotional credits
   - Incentive payments
   - Manual credits

#### Payout Processing
1. Batch created for payout date
2. Orders aggregated by restaurant
3. Commission calculated
4. Adjustments applied
5. Payout amount calculated
6. Processed via Moov ACH
7. Restaurant notified
8. Funds deposited

### 17.3 Payout Reconciliation

#### Daily Reconciliation
- Moov transactions vs. platform records
- Payout batches verified
- Amounts matched
- Discrepancies investigated

#### Reporting
- Payout summaries

## 18. Financial Flow & Economics

### 18.1 Order Economics Breakdown

#### Standard Order Example ($30.00 subtotal)

**Customer Pays:**
- Food subtotal: $30.00
- Service fee (10%): $3.00
- Delivery fee: $4.49
- Tip: $5.00
- **Total: $42.49**

**Platform Revenue:**
- Commission (15% Silver): $4.50
- Service fee: $3.00
- Delivery fee: $4.49
- **Gross Revenue: $11.99**
- Less: Processing fees (~$1.01)
- Less: Driver base pay ($3.14)
- **Net Revenue: ~$7.84**

**Restaurant Receives:**
- Gross sales: $30.00
- Less commission: $4.50
- **Net: $25.50**

**Driver Earns:**
- Base pay: $3.14
- Tip: $5.00
- **Total: $8.14**

### 18.2 Revenue Streams

#### Primary Revenue
1. **Restaurant Commissions:**
   - 8-18% of order subtotals
   - Largest revenue component
   - Varies by tier

2. **Service Fees:**
   - 10% of subtotals (standard)
   - 5-7% for CraveMore members
   - Platform revenue

3. **Delivery Fees:**
   - $2.99 base + $0.50/mile
   - Waived for CraveMore members
   - Platform revenue (70% after driver pay)

#### Secondary Revenue
1. **CraveMore Subscriptions:**
   - $8.99/month or $90/year
   - Lifetime: $249 (limited)
   - Recurring revenue

2. **Processing Fee Margin:**
   - Difference between charged and actual costs
   - Minimal, but contributes

### 18.3 Cost Structure

#### Variable Costs
1. **Payment Processing:**
   - ~2.7% for cards
   - ~0.5% for ACH
   - Per-transaction cost

2. **Driver Base Pay:**
   - 70% of delivery fees
   - Variable by order volume

3. **Customer Support:**
   - Scales with order volume
   - Technology-enabled efficiency

#### Fixed Costs
1. **Technology Infrastructure:**
   - Cloud hosting
   - Software licenses
   - Development costs

2. **Operations:**
   - Staff salaries
   - Office expenses
   - Marketing

3. **Compliance:**
   - Legal
   - Insurance
   - Regulatory

### 18.4 Unit Economics

#### Per-Order Economics
- **Gross Revenue:** $11.99
- **Variable Costs:** ~$4.15 (processing + driver pay)
- **Contribution Margin:** ~$7.84
- **Fixed Cost Allocation:** Varies
- **Net Profit:** Positive at scale

#### Scale Economics
- Fixed costs spread across more orders
- Better processing rates at volume
- Operational efficiency improvements
- Profitability improves with scale
