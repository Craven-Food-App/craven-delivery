# Executive Sponsor Workflow for Intern Program

**Document ID:** SOP-INTERN-009

**Department:** Executive Leadership  
**Process Owner:** Executive Sponsor  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly  

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Define the role and responsibilities of Executive Sponsors in the intern program, providing strategic guidance and mentorship to high-potential interns.

---

## 2. EXECUTIVE SPONSOR ROLE

**Purpose:**
- Provide strategic mentorship
- Offer leadership perspective
- Support career development
- Approve high-level conversions
- Champion intern program

**Time Commitment:**
- 2-4 hours per month per intern
- Quarterly meetings
- Conversion reviews
- Ad-hoc guidance

---

## 3. WHEN EXECUTIVE SPONSORSHIP IS ASSIGNED

**Criteria for Executive Sponsorship:**
- High-potential interns
- Leadership track programs
- Technical excellence programs
- Interns in strategic roles
- Conversion candidates for senior positions

**Typical Sponsors:**
- CEO: Leadership track interns
- CTO: Technical/engineering interns
- CFO: Finance/analytics interns
- COO: Operations interns
- CMO: Marketing interns

---

## 4. RESPONSIBILITIES

### Strategic Guidance
- Share company vision and strategy
- Explain executive decision-making
- Provide industry insights
- Discuss career pathways

### Mentorship
- Quarterly 1:1 meetings
- Career advice
- Network introductions
- Leadership development

### Evaluation
- Review performance at key milestones
- Provide input on conversion decisions
- Approve senior-level conversions

### Advocacy
- Champion intern program internally
- Support intern initiatives
- Remove organizational blockers

---

## 5. PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Sponsorship Assignment

**Who:** HR Director  
**When:** Within first 30 days of intern program  
**Time:** 15 minutes

1. HR identifies high-potential interns
2. Matches with appropriate executive
3. Sends sponsorship request to executive
4. Executive accepts or declines
5. If accepted, introduction is scheduled

---

### Step 2: Initial Meeting

**Who:** Executive Sponsor + Intern  
**When:** Within 2 weeks of assignment  
**Time:** 45 minutes

**Meeting Agenda:**

**1. Introductions (10 min)**
- Executive shares background
- Intern shares background
- Establish rapport

**2. Program Overview (10 min)**
- Review intern's role and goals
- Discuss learning plan
- Explain sponsorship purpose

**3. Expectations Setting (15 min)**
- Meeting frequency (quarterly)
- Communication preferences
- What intern can expect from sponsor
- What sponsor expects from intern

**4. Career Discussion (10 min)**
- Intern's career aspirations
- Areas of interest
- Skills to develop
- Long-term goals

**5. Next Steps (5 min)**
- Schedule next meeting
- Set initial goals
- Exchange contact information

---

### Step 3: Quarterly Check-Ins

**Who:** Executive Sponsor + Intern  
**When:** Every 90 days  
**Time:** 30-45 minutes

**Q1 Meeting (Day 30):**
- How is onboarding going?
- Early impressions
- Challenges faced
- Adjustment to company culture

**Q2 Meeting (Day 120):**
- Review progress
- Discuss projects
- Provide strategic feedback
- Discuss career interests

**Q3 Meeting (Day 210):**
- Assess growth
- Discuss conversion interest
- Provide guidance on next steps
- Network introductions

**Q4 Meeting (if applicable):**
- Long-term planning
- Career development
- Continued relationship

---

### Step 4: Conversion Review

**Who:** Executive Sponsor  
**When:** When intern is eligible for conversion  
**Time:** 1-2 hours

**Review Process:**

1. **Receive Conversion Request**
   - Manager submits conversion request
   - HR sends to executive sponsor
   - Includes performance data

2. **Review Materials**
   - Performance evaluations
   - Task completion history
   - Manager recommendation
   - Proposed position and salary

3. **Interview Intern (Optional)**
   - Assess readiness
   - Discuss career goals
   - Evaluate cultural fit
   - Gauge commitment

4. **Make Decision**
   - Approve
   - Approve with conditions
   - Deny with feedback

5. **Provide Feedback**
   - Document decision
   - Explain reasoning
   - Offer guidance

---

### Step 5: Ongoing Support

**Throughout Program:**

**Ad-Hoc Guidance:**
- Respond to intern questions
- Provide advice when requested
- Make introductions as appropriate

**Remove Blockers:**
- Help navigate organizational challenges
- Facilitate cross-functional collaboration
- Advocate for intern's projects

**Visibility:**
- Invite to strategic meetings (when appropriate)
- Include in leadership events
- Provide exposure to senior leadership

---

## 6. BEST PRACTICES

### DO:
✅ Be accessible and responsive  
✅ Share real experiences and lessons learned  
✅ Make meaningful introductions  
✅ Provide honest, constructive feedback  
✅ Advocate for intern's growth  
✅ Follow through on commitments  
✅ Celebrate successes  

### DON'T:
❌ Micromanage day-to-day work  
❌ Bypass direct manager  
❌ Make promises you can't keep  
❌ Share confidential information inappropriately  
❌ Show favoritism  
❌ Forget scheduled meetings

## 10. CONVERSION APPROVAL AUTHORITY

**Executive Sponsor Approval Required For:**
- Senior-level positions
- Leadership track conversions
- Equity grants
- Salary above standard range
- New position creation

**Approval Criteria:**
- Performance excellence
- Leadership potential
- Cultural fit
- Strategic value
- Business need

**Decision Timeline:**
- Review within 48 hours
- Provide decision within 5 business days
- Document reasoning

## 11. SUCCESS METRICS

**For Executive Sponsors:**
- Meeting completion rate (target: 100%)
- Intern satisfaction with sponsorship (target: 4.5+/5.0)
- Conversion rate of sponsored interns (target: 70%+)
- Retention of converted interns (target: 95%+)

**For Program:**
- Executive sponsor participation rate
- Quality of mentorship (intern feedback)
- Impact on intern performance
- Conversion success rate

---

## 12. TIME COMMITMENT SUMMARY

**Per Intern Per Quarter:**
- Initial meeting: 45 minutes
- Quarterly check-in: 45 minutes
- Ad-hoc emails/calls: 30 minutes
- Conversion review: 1-2 hours (if applicable)

**Total: 2-4 hours per month**

**For Multiple Interns:**
- Typically 1-3 interns per executive
- Total: 6-12 hours per month

---

## 13. WHEN TO ESCALATE

**Escalate to HR if:**
- Performance concerns
- Behavioral issues
- Interpersonal conflicts
- Policy violations
- Intern requests termination
- Manager-intern conflict

**Don't Handle Directly:**
- HR issues
- Compensation negotiations
- Disciplinary actions
- Legal matters

---

## 14. END OF SPONSORSHIP

**Program Completion:**
1. Final meeting with intern
2. Provide recommendation letter (if requested)
3. Offer to stay in touch
4. Add to professional network
5. Invite to alumni events

**If Converted to FTE:**
1. Congratulate intern
2. Transition to ongoing mentorship (optional)
3. Continue relationship as appropriate

**If Not Converted:**
1. Provide constructive feedback
2. Offer career advice
3. Make external introductions if appropriate
4. Maintain positive relationship

---
