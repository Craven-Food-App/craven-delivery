# SOP-006: Converting Interns to Full-Time Employees

**Document ID:** SOP-INTERN-006  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** December 18, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Process Owner:** HR Director  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly  

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Define the process for converting successful interns to full-time employees, ensuring fair evaluation and smooth transition.

---

## 2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Interns eligible for conversion must meet ALL requirements:

### Minimum Requirements
- ✅ Completed 90+ days in program
- ✅ Performance score ≥ 4.0/5.0
- ✅ All evaluations completed
- ✅ All required modules passed
- ✅ Attendance rate ≥ 95%
- ✅ No disciplinary actions
- ✅ Manager recommendation

### Additional Considerations
- Quality of work
- Cultural fit
- Team feedback
- Growth potential
- Business need for position

---

## 3. CONVERSION TIMELINE

```
Day 60:  Initial conversion discussion
Day 75:  Eligibility assessment
Day 80:  Approval process begins
Day 85:  Offer extended (if approved)
Day 90:  Program ends / FTE starts
```

---

## 4. PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Initial Conversion Discussion

**Who:** Manager + Intern  
**When:** Day 60  
**Time:** 30 minutes

1. Manager initiates conversation
2. Discuss intern's interest in full-time role
3. Explain conversion process
4. Review eligibility criteria
5. Set expectations for evaluation

**If intern interested:**
- Continue to Step 2

**If intern not interested:**
- Document decision
- Proceed with standard exit process (SOP-INTERN-007)

---

### Step 2: Eligibility Assessment

**Who:** Manager + HR  
**When:** Day 75  
**Time:** 1 hour

1. Manager logs into system
2. Navigate to **Conversion Pathway** for intern
3. System auto-calculates eligibility:
   - Days completed: [Auto]
   - Performance score: [Auto from evaluations]
   - Modules completed: [Auto]
   - Attendance rate: [Auto from time logs]
   - Disciplinary actions: [Auto]

4. Manager adds:
   - Recommendation (Yes/No)
   - Justification
   - Proposed position
   - Proposed salary range
   - Start date

5. Click **Submit for Review**

**Eligibility Status:**
- ✅ **Eligible** - Meets all criteria
- ⚠️ **Conditional** - Meets most criteria, needs approval
- ❌ **Not Eligible** - Does not meet criteria

---

### Step 3: Approval Workflow

**Multi-Step Approval Process:**

#### Level 1: Manager Approval
**Who:** Direct Manager  
**When:** Day 76  
**Action:** Approve or Deny with justification

#### Level 2: Department Head Approval
**Who:** Department Head  
**When:** Day 77  
**Action:** Review and approve/deny

#### Level 3: HR Director Approval
**Who:** HR Director  
**When:** Day 78  
**Action:** 
- Verify eligibility
- Confirm budget availability
- Review compensation
- Approve or deny

#### Level 4: Executive Approval (if required)
**Who:** Executive Sponsor or C-Level  
**When:** Day 79  
**Required for:**
- Positions above entry-level
- Salary above threshold
- New position creation

#### Level 5: CEO Approval (if required)
**Who:** CEO  
**When:** Day 80  
**Required for:**
- Leadership track positions
- Equity grants
- Executive roles

**Each approver receives notification and must act within 24 hours**

---

### Step 4: Offer Preparation

**Who:** HR Coordinator  
**When:** Upon final approval  
**Time:** 2 hours

1. Prepare offer letter including:
   - Position title
   - Department
   - Start date
   - Salary
   - Benefits summary
   - Equity (if applicable)
   - Reporting structure
   - Work location/schedule

2. Route for signatures:
   - HR Director
   - CEO (for exec roles)

3. Prepare offer packet:
   - Offer letter
   - Benefits guide
   - Employee handbook
   - New hire paperwork

---

### Step 5: Offer Presentation

**Who:** Manager + HR  
**When:** Day 85  
**Time:** 45 minutes

**Meeting Agenda:**
1. Congratulate intern
2. Present offer letter
3. Review compensation and benefits
4. Answer questions
5. Explain next steps
6. Set decision deadline (typically 5-7 days)

**Provide:**
- Written offer letter
- Benefits summary
- Time to review and ask questions

---

### Step 6: Offer Response

**Intern has 3 options:**

#### Option A: Accept Offer
1. Intern signs offer letter
2. Returns to HR within deadline
3. HR processes acceptance:
   - Update employee status
   - Schedule start date
   - Initiate onboarding
   - Close intern program record

#### Option B: Negotiate Offer
1. Intern requests discussion
2. HR reviews request
3. Determine if negotiation possible
4. Present revised offer or explain constraints
5. Intern accepts or declines

#### Option C: Decline Offer
1. Intern declines in writing
2. HR documents reason (if provided)
3. Proceed with exit process
4. Maintain positive relationship

---

### Step 7: Transition to FTE

**Who:** HR + Manager  
**When:** Between acceptance and start date  
**Time:** Varies

**HR Actions:**
1. Update HRIS system:
   - Change status from Intern to Employee
   - Update position and salary
   - Enroll in benefits
   - Update access permissions

2. Prepare new employee onboarding:
   - Benefits enrollment
   - Tax withholding updates
   - Direct deposit setup
   - Equipment (if needed)

3. Communicate transition:
   - Announce to team
   - Update org chart
   - Update email signature/title

**Manager Actions:**
1. Plan first week as FTE:
   - New responsibilities
   - New projects
   - Team integration

2. Set 30-60-90 day goals

3. Schedule regular 1:1s

---

### Step 8: First Day as FTE

**Who:** Manager + HR  
**When:** FTE start date  
**Time:** 2 hours

**Morning:**
1. Welcome as full-time employee
2. Review new role and responsibilities
3. Discuss career development
4. Set performance expectations

**Afternoon:**
1. Complete new hire paperwork
2. Benefits enrollment
3. Update systems and access
4. Team celebration (optional)

---

### Step 9: 30-Day FTE Check-In

**Who:** Manager + New Employee  
**When:** 30 days after conversion  
**Time:** 30 minutes

**Discussion Topics:**
1. How is the transition going?
2. Any challenges?
3. Review 30-day goals
4. Set 60-day goals
5. Discuss career development

---

## 5. CONVERSION DECISION MATRIX

| Criteria | Weight | Minimum Score |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Performance Score | 30% | 4.0/5.0 |
| Manager Recommendation | 25% | Required |
| Cultural Fit | 20% | Strong |
| Technical Skills | 15% | Proficient |
| Growth Potential | 10% | High |

**Overall Score Required:** 80%+

---

## 6. COMPENSATION GUIDELINES

**Entry-Level Positions:**
- Base salary: Market rate for role
- Benefits: Standard package
- PTO: Standard accrual
- Equity: Per company policy

**Conversion Salary Factors:**
- Market rate for position
- Internal equity
- Intern performance
- Budget constraints
- Geographic location

**Typical Conversion Salary Range:**
- Entry-Level: $50,000 - $70,000
- Mid-Level: $70,000 - $90,000
- Senior-Level: $90,000 - $120,000

*(Adjust based on role, location, and market)*

---

## 7. CONVERSION RATE GOALS

**Target Metrics:**
- Conversion rate: 40-60% of eligible interns
- Offer acceptance rate: 85%+
- 1-year retention: 90%+
- Performance in first year: 4.0+ average

---

## 8. IF CONVERSION IS NOT APPROVED

**Manager Actions:**
1. Meet with intern privately
2. Explain decision (if appropriate)
3. Provide constructive feedback
4. Offer to be reference
5. Maintain positive relationship
6. Proceed with exit process

**HR Actions:**
1. Document decision and reasoning
2. Update intern record
3. Initiate exit process
4. Provide career resources
5. Add to alumni network

## 10. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Intern eligible but no open position | Create position if business justifies, or add to waitlist |
| Budget not available | Explore other departments, delay start date, or decline |
| Intern wants different role | Assess fit for other roles, may require additional interviews |
| Salary expectations too high | Explain compensation philosophy, show market data |
| Intern has other offers | Move quickly, highlight unique value proposition |
| Manager wants to convert but intern not eligible | Review criteria, consider exception process if warranted |
