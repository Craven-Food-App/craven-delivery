# Academic Credit Management for Interns

**Document ID:** SOP-INTERN-005  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** December 18, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Process Owner:** Academic Coordinator  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly  

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Define the process for managing academic credit partnerships with educational institutions, ensuring interns receive proper credit for their work experience.

---

## 2. SCOPE

**Applies to:** Interns enrolled in academic programs seeking course credit  
**Covers:** Learning agreements, time tracking, evaluations, grade submission

---

## 3. ELIGIBILITY

Interns eligible for academic credit must:
- Be enrolled in an accredited educational institution
- Have institution approval for internship credit
- Complete minimum 120 hours of work
- Meet program requirements
- Submit required documentation

---

## 4. PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Initial Setup (Before Start Date)

**Who:** HR Coordinator + Intern  
**Time:** 30 minutes

1. Intern provides:
   - Institution name
   - Academic advisor contact
   - Course code/number
   - Credit hours requested
   - Institution requirements

2. HR creates academic credit record in system
3. HR contacts academic advisor to confirm requirements

---

### Step 2: Learning Agreement

**Who:** HR Coordinator  
**Time:** 1 hour

1. Create learning agreement including:
   - Learning objectives
   - Work responsibilities
   - Evaluation criteria
   - Time requirements
   - Deliverables

2. Obtain signatures:
   - Intern
   - Academic Advisor
   - Intern Supervisor
   - HR Director

3. Upload signed agreement to system

---

### Step 3: Time Tracking

**Who:** Intern (ongoing)

1. Log all hours in Intern Portal
2. Categorize activities
3. Submit weekly for approval
4. System tracks cumulative hours

**Minimum Hours Required:** 120 hours (typically 3 credits)

---

### Step 4: Progress Reports

**Who:** Supervisor  
**When:** Mid-term and Final

1. Complete progress report form
2. Assess learning objective achievement
3. Provide narrative feedback
4. Submit to academic advisor

---

### Step 5: Final Evaluation & Grade

**Who:** Supervisor + HR  
**When:** End of program

1. Complete final evaluation
2. Verify hours completed
3. Assess overall performance
4. Assign grade (if required)
5. Submit to institution

**Grading Scale:**
- A: Exceptional (5.0 performance)
- B: Above Average (4.0-4.9)
- C: Satisfactory (3.0-3.9)
- F: Unsatisfactory (< 3.0)

---

### Step 6: Documentation

**Who:** HR Coordinator

1. Maintain all documents:
   - Learning agreement
   - Time logs
   - Progress reports
   - Evaluations
   - Correspondence

2. Provide copies to:
   - Intern
   - Academic institution
   - Company records

---

## 5. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

- [ ] Learning Agreement
- [ ] Internship Offer Letter
- [ ] Mid-Term Progress Report
- [ ] Final Evaluation
- [ ] Time Log Summary
- [ ] Grade Submission Form

---

## 6. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Institution requirements unclear | Contact academic advisor directly |
| Intern not meeting hour requirements | Extend program or adjust credit hours |
| Academic advisor not responding | Escalate to department chair |
| Grade dispute | Review evaluation with intern and advisor |
