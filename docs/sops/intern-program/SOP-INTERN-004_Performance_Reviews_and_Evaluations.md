# Intern Performance Reviews & Evaluations

**Document ID:** SOP-INTERN-004  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** December 18, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Process Owner:** Hiring Manager  
**Review Frequency:** Quarterly  

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Define the process for conducting fair, consistent, and developmental performance reviews for interns throughout their program.

---

## 2. EVALUATION SCHEDULE

| Evaluation Type | Timing | Purpose | Conducted By |
|----------------|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Onboarding** | Day 14 | Assess initial integration | Manager |
| **Mid-Term** | Day 45 | Check progress, adjust goals | Manager |
| **Final** | Day 90 | Comprehensive review | Manager + HR |
| **Quarterly** | Every 90 days | Ongoing programs | Manager |
| **Conversion** | Before conversion | Assess FTE readiness | Manager + Executive |

---

## 3. EVALUATION CRITERIA

### Core Competencies (Rated 1-5)

**1. Technical Skills**
- Proficiency in required tools/technologies
- Quality of work output
- Problem-solving ability
- Learning agility

**2. Communication**
- Clarity in written communication
- Effectiveness in verbal communication
- Active listening
- Asking appropriate questions

**3. Teamwork & Collaboration**
- Works well with others
- Contributes to team goals
- Respects diverse perspectives
- Shares knowledge

**4. Initiative & Proactivity**
- Takes ownership of work
- Seeks out learning opportunities
- Identifies and suggests improvements
- Self-directed learning

**5. Quality & Attention to Detail**
- Thoroughness of work
- Accuracy and precision
- Follows standards and guidelines
- Tests and validates work

**6. Time Management**
- Meets deadlines
- Prioritizes effectively
- Manages workload
- Communicates delays proactively

**7. Adaptability**
- Handles feedback constructively
- Adapts to changing priorities
- Learns from mistakes
- Embraces new challenges

## 4. PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Prepare for Evaluation

**Who:** Manager  
**When:** 1 week before evaluation  
**Time:** 30 minutes

1. Review intern's performance data:
   - [ ] Tasks completed
   - [ ] Deliverables submitted
   - [ ] Time logs
   - [ ] Feedback received
   - [ ] Attendance record
   - [ ] Learning modules completed

2. Gather input from others:
   - Mentor feedback
   - Team member observations
   - Project lead comments

3. Review previous evaluation (if applicable):
   - Goals set
   - Progress made
   - Areas of improvement

4. Prepare evaluation notes:
   - Specific examples of strengths
   - Specific examples of areas to improve
   - Suggested goals for next period

---

### Step 2: Intern Self-Evaluation

**Who:** Intern  
**When:** 3 days before evaluation  
**Time:** 30 minutes

1. Log in to Intern Portal
2. Navigate to **Evaluations** > **Self-Evaluation**
3. Complete self-assessment:
   - Rate yourself on core competencies (1-5)
   - List accomplishments
   - Identify challenges
   - Describe learnings
   - Set personal goals

4. Submit self-evaluation

**Questions to Consider:**
- What am I most proud of?
- What was most challenging?
- What did I learn?
- Where do I want to improve?
- What support do I need?

---

### Step 3: Conduct Evaluation Meeting

**Who:** Manager + Intern  
**When:** Scheduled evaluation date  
**Time:** 45-60 minutes

**Meeting Structure:**

**1. Opening (5 min)**
- Set positive tone
- Explain evaluation purpose
- Review agenda

**2. Intern Self-Reflection (10 min)**
- Intern shares self-evaluation
- Discuss accomplishments
- Discuss challenges
- Listen actively

**3. Manager Feedback (20 min)**
- Review each competency
- Provide specific examples
- Highlight strengths
- Discuss areas for growth
- Use "sandwich" approach (positive-constructive-positive)

**4. Goal Setting (15 min)**
- Review previous goals (if applicable)
- Set new goals for next period
- Ensure goals are SMART:
  - Specific
  - Measurable
  - Achievable
  - Relevant
  - Time-bound

**5. Development Plan (10 min)**
- Identify learning opportunities
- Assign training/resources
- Discuss career interests
- Plan next steps

**6. Closing (5 min)**
- Summarize key points
- Answer questions
- Provide encouragement
- Schedule follow-up

---

### Step 4: Document Evaluation

**Who:** Manager  
**When:** Within 24 hours of meeting  
**Time:** 20 minutes

1. Log in to Manager Portal
2. Navigate to **My Interns** > [Intern Name] > **Evaluations**
3. Click **Create Evaluation**
4. Select evaluation type
5. Complete evaluation form:

```yaml
Evaluation Period: [Start Date] to [End Date]

COMPETENCY RATINGS (1-5 scale):
Technical Skills: [Rating]
Communication: [Rating]
Teamwork: [Rating]
Initiative: [Rating]
Quality: [Rating]
Time Management: [Rating]
Adaptability: [Rating]

Overall Score: [Auto-calculated average]

STRENGTHS:
[Specific examples of what intern does well]

AREAS FOR IMPROVEMENT:
[Specific areas with actionable suggestions]

ACCOMPLISHMENTS THIS PERIOD:
[List key achievements]

GOALS FOR NEXT PERIOD:
Goal 1: [SMART goal]
Goal 2: [SMART goal]
Goal 3: [SMART goal]

DEVELOPMENT PLAN:
[Training, resources, opportunities]

MANAGER COMMENTS:
[Additional observations and recommendations]

INTERN COMMENTS:
[Space for intern to add comments after review]
```

6. Click **Submit Evaluation**
7. System sends copy to intern and HR

---

### Step 5: Intern Acknowledgment

**Who:** Intern  
**When:** Within 3 days of receiving evaluation  
**Time:** 10 minutes

1. Review evaluation in Intern Portal
2. Add comments if desired
3. Click **Acknowledge Evaluation**
4. If concerns, can request HR review

---

### Step 6: Follow-Up Actions

**Who:** Manager  
**When:** Ongoing  
**Time:** Varies

1. Schedule regular check-ins (weekly/bi-weekly)
2. Monitor progress on goals
3. Provide ongoing feedback
4. Adjust support as needed
5. Document progress in activity log

## 5. RATING SCALE DEFINITIONS

| Rating | Label | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **5** | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds expectations; demonstrates mastery |
| **4** | Exceeds Expectations | Regularly performs above standard; shows strong capability |
| **3** | Meets Expectations | Consistently meets standards; solid performance |
| **2** | Needs Improvement | Sometimes meets standards; requires development |
| **1** | Unsatisfactory | Rarely meets standards; significant improvement needed |

**Note:** For interns, ratings of 3-4 are typical and positive. Rating of 5 is rare and reserved for truly exceptional performance.

---

## 6. FEEDBACK BEST PRACTICES

### DO:
✅ Be specific with examples  
✅ Focus on behaviors, not personality  
✅ Balance positive and constructive  
✅ Provide actionable suggestions  
✅ Listen actively  
✅ Be empathetic  
✅ End on a positive note  

### DON'T:
❌ Use vague language  
❌ Make it personal  
❌ Only focus on negatives  
❌ Compare to other interns  
❌ Surprise intern (give ongoing feedback)  
❌ Be defensive  
❌ Rush the conversation  

---

## 7. DIFFICULT CONVERSATIONS

### If Performance is Below Expectations:

**Approach:**
1. State concerns clearly and specifically
2. Provide concrete examples
3. Explain impact
4. Ask for intern's perspective
5. Collaboratively create improvement plan
6. Set clear expectations and timeline
7. Offer support and resources
8. Document conversation
9. Schedule follow-up check-ins

**Performance Improvement Plan (PIP):**
- Specific areas needing improvement
- Measurable goals
- Timeline (typically 30 days)
- Support provided
- Consequences if not improved
- Weekly check-ins

## 9. CONVERSION EVALUATION

For interns being considered for full-time employment:

**Additional Assessment Areas:**
- Cultural fit
- Long-term potential
- Leadership capability
- Strategic thinking
- Business acumen

**Additional Evaluators:**
- Department Head
- HR Director
- Executive Sponsor (if applicable)

**Process:**
1. Manager completes standard evaluation
2. Department Head provides input
3. HR reviews performance history
4. Executive Sponsor (if applicable) interviews intern
5. Hiring committee makes decision
6. See SOP-INTERN-006 for full conversion process

## 10. METRICS & REPORTING

**Track:**
- Average evaluation scores by intern
- Evaluation completion rate (target: 100%)
- Time to complete evaluations (target: < 48 hours after meeting)
- Goal achievement rate
- Improvement trends

**Reports:**
- Individual intern performance dashboard
- Cohort performance comparison
- Evaluation completion status
- Performance trends over time

---

## 11. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Intern disagrees with evaluation | Listen to concerns, provide specific examples, involve HR if needed |
| Manager hasn't completed evaluation | HR sends reminder, escalate to department head |
| Evaluation scores inconsistent | Calibration session with managers, review rating definitions |
| Intern not improving after feedback | Create formal PIP, increase support, consider program fit |
