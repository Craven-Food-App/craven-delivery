Executive Summary

This document outlines the remote outreach script for engaging Schmucker's regarding delivery platform commission reduction opportunities. The approach emphasizes financial value, risk mitigation, and operational simplicity.

Opening Script

Credible, Disarming Introduction

"Hi, this is Justin Sweet with Crave'n. I'm calling from New York, so I'll be respectful of your time and keep this tight. I work on the financial and partner strategy side for Crave'n, and I wanted to connect with whoever oversees delivery and third-party platform decisions for Schmucker's."

Note: If transferred or contact confirmed, proceed to Core Pitch.

Core Pitch

Financial Value Proposition

"The reason for the call is straightforward. We work with high-volume local restaurants to materially reduce third-party delivery commission leakage without changing how they operate.

Schmucker's is a well-known Toledo operator with strong dine-in volume. Based on local diner benchmarks, you're likely in the range of two million dollars annually, with roughly twenty percent flowing through delivery platforms.

At the industry-standard thirty percent commission, that equates to approximately one hundred twenty thousand dollars per year in platform fees on delivery alone.

Crave'n operates at a flat fifteen percent. On the same order volume, same menu pricing, same kitchen flow, that's roughly sixty thousand dollars annually retained by the restaurant — about five thousand dollars per month."

Action: Pause. Let the math work.

Value Differentiation

Authority Positioning

"We're not positioning ourselves as another national app. Crave'n is built specifically to support local operators by removing algorithmic pressure, forced discounting, and long-term exclusivity. This is a margin-protection model, not a marketing experiment."

Risk Mitigation

Close Lever

"We structure this as a thirty-day, non-exclusive pilot. Flat fifteen percent. If it doesn't perform financially or operationally, there's no obligation to continue."

Call to Action

Remote-Friendly Close

"What I'd recommend is a brief fifteen-minute call where I can walk you through a Schmucker's-specific financial breakdown and answer questions. If it doesn't pencil out, we part ways professionally.

Would a short call later this week or early next week work better on your end?"

Alternative Scripts

Ultra-Short Version

Use when: Contact is time-constrained or requests brevity.

"Understood — one sentence version: we reduce delivery commissions from thirty percent to fifteen percent and put roughly five thousand dollars a month back into Schmucker's, with no contract. If that's worth a fifteen-minute review, I'm happy to walk through the numbers."

Internal Guidance

Key Principles (Do Not Say Aloud)

  • Lead with numbers, not geography
  • Being in New York adds perceived financial seniority, not distance
  • Avoid "startup language" entirely
  • Let silence do the work after the commission math

Communication Best Practices

  1. Financial Focus: Always lead with concrete financial impact
  2. Authority Positioning: Leverage New York location as credibility signal
  3. Language Discipline: Maintain enterprise tone; avoid startup terminology
  4. Strategic Pauses: Use silence strategically after presenting financial data
  5. Risk Removal: Emphasize non-exclusive, no-obligation structure
