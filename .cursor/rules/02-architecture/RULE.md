Follow a service-oriented architecture.

Guidelines:
- Clear separation between API, business logic, and persistence
- Stateless services where possible
- External integrations isolated behind adapters
- No business logic in controllers or routes

When proposing changes:
- Describe impact on existing services
- Identify migration or backward-compatibility concerns
- Call out scaling or cost implications

Avoid monolithic coupling unless explicitly justified.
