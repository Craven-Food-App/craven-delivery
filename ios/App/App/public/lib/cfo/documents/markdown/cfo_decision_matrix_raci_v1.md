## 1. Purpose Of This Matrix

Defines decision-making authority for CFO and cross-functional executives. Ensures alignment, accountability, and clarity at scale.

---

## 2. RACI Legend

**D – Decision Authority:** Final decision power  

**A – Accountable:** Owns the outcome  

**R – Responsible:** Performs the work  

**C – Consulted:** Provides input  

**I – Informed:** Updated afterward

## 4. Rules Of Engagement

### CFO Must Escalate To CEO When:

- Cash or runway materially deviates  

- Control or compliance risk emerges  

- Large vendor exposure or liability appears  

- A decision impacts multiple departments  

### CFO Independent Authority Includes:

- Reporting cadence  

- Internal controls  

- Financial system design  

- Closing & reconciliation  

- Expense governance
