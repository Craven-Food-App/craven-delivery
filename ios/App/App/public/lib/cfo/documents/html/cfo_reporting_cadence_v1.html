1. Purpose Of This Document

This Reporting Cadence outlines the exact frequency, structure, and expectations for all financial and operational reporting performed by the Chief Financial Officer. It establishes a consistent, reliable rhythm that mirrors Fortune 500 financial operating standards while supporting Crave'n's high-growth environment.

2. Reporting Cadence Overview

The CFO must deliver accurate, timely, and decision-ready financial insights across the following standardized reporting intervals:

  • Daily
  • Weekly
  • Monthly
  • Quarterly
  • Annual
  • Ad-Hoc (CEO-requested)

3. Daily Reporting Requirements

3.1 Daily Liquidity Status

  • Bank balances
  • Incoming funds
  • Outgoing payments
  • Variance vs cash forecast

3.2 Financial System Health

  • Accounting system sync verification
  • Reconciliation status
  • Any anomalies requiring escalation

3.3 Daily Notes To CEO (If Needed)

  • Unexpected withdrawals or deposits
  • Vendor issues
  • Urgent risks or concerns

4. Weekly Reporting Requirements

Weekly reporting is the foundation of Crave'n's financial visibility. Unless otherwise noted, all weekly reports must be delivered every Friday by 3:00 PM ET.

4.1 Weekly Finance Packet To CEO

  • Cash position
  • Weekly burn rate
  • Cash runway (12-week projection)
  • Top vendor spend and upcoming renewals
  • Variance to weekly budget
  • Operational notes and flags
  • Action items for next week

4.2 Department Spend Summary

  • COO: market operations, payouts, logistics costs
  • CTO: cloud, platform, vendor infrastructure
  • CXO: acquisition costs, promotions

4.3 Vendor Exposure Review

  • Upcoming renewals
  • Contracts above threshold
  • High-risk vendors

5. Monthly Reporting Requirements

Monthly reporting must be delivered no later than the 5th business day following month-end. CFO must escalate delays to the CEO immediately.

5.1 Monthly Financial Statements

  • Income Statement
  • Balance Sheet
  • Cash Flow Statement
  • Statement of changes in cash

5.2 Monthly Unit Economics

  • Gross margin by market
  • Contribution margin analysis
  • Driver payout ratios
  • Customer acquisition vs retention cost

5.3 Monthly Variance Report

  • Budget vs Actual
  • Forecast vs Actual
  • Major variance explanations
  • Corrective actions

5.4 CEO Monthly Commentary

The CFO must produce an executive summary narrative that explains performance trends, risks, opportunities, and directional recommendations.

6. Quarterly Reporting Requirements

Quarterly reporting forms the basis of strategic alignment and must be delivered by the 10th business day following quarter-end.

6.1 Quarterly Strategy Packet

  • Updated 3-year financial plan
  • Pricing and fee structure analyses
  • Payout structure assessment
  • Gross margin trend review
  • Market expansion financial readiness

6.2 Quarterly KPI Dashboard

  • CAC and LTV
  • Delivery cost per order
  • Gross profit per market
  • Driver retention and incentive cost

6.3 Quarterly Compliance Review

  • Regulatory filings
  • Tax reporting
  • Internal controls status
  • Risk and exposure updates

7. Annual Reporting Requirements

  • Annual budget and operating plan
  • Formal 3-year long-range plan
  • Year-end financial statements
  • Annual cash flow projection
  • Year-end investor packet

8. Ad-Hoc Reporting

The CEO may request special or urgent reporting on any financial matter, including:

  • Market expansion analysis
  • Pricing and fee sensitivity analysis
  • Crisis financial modeling
  • Vendor deep-dive review

9. Reporting Accuracy Standards

  • Numbers must reconcile to systems of record
  • All assumptions must be documented
  • Forecasts must have supporting schedules
  • No rounding or simplification without disclosure
  • No unverified data allowed

10. Escalation Rules

  • Missed reporting deadlines → immediate CEO escalation
  • Material variance → CEO must be notified same-day
  • Data integrity issues → immediate root-cause analysis
  • Unexpected cash outflows → flag within 24 hours
