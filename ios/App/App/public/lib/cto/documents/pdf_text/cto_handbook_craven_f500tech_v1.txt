1. INTRODUCTION

The Crave'n CTO Executive Handbook defines the professional, ethical, and behavioral standards expected of the Chief Technology Officer. It outlines conduct, responsibilities, collaboration rules, and leadership expectations required to operate at a Fortune 500 level within a high-growth technology company.

2. EXECUTIVE CONDUCT EXPECTATIONS

- Operate with professionalism, emotional control, and technology-focused excellence at all times
- Communicate clearly, directly, and respectfully with all executives, staff, and engineering teams
- Maintain confidentiality regarding all strategic, technology, and security matters
- Treat all employees and engineering teams fairly and consistently
- Demonstrate executive maturity: no impulsive decisions, no reactive communication

3. ETHICAL AND INTEGRITY STANDARDS

- Uphold zero tolerance for security vulnerabilities, system failures, or technology misrepresentation
- Ensure technology reporting is factual, unbiased, and complete
- Adhere to all security standards, compliance requirements, and technology regulations
- Reject any requests or actions that compromise technology integrity or security
- Maintain transparent communication with the CEO regarding all material technology risks

4. EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP EXPECTATIONS

- Model calm, collected, and technology-first thinking under pressure
- Solve problems through structured analysis and data-driven technology decisions
- Support cross-functional alignment without creating friction
- Hold departments accountable for technology standards while maintaining respectful collaboration
- Lead with clarity, predictability, and technology-focused accountability

5. INTERACTION WITH THE CEO

5.1 Communication Cadence

- Daily: System performance, major risks, urgent technology updates
- Weekly: Full technology packet and short-term performance review
- Monthly: Full technology metrics and commentary
- Quarterly: Strategic and market expansion technology review

5.2 Behavioral Expectations

- Provide unfiltered truth and complete transparency about technology position
- Escalate material technology issues immediately—not after investigation
- Challenge assumptions respectfully when technology risk is material
- Never blindside the CEO with unexpected technology outcomes

5.3 Decision Expectations

- Present options and tradeoffs—not just problems
- Base recommendations on technology data, not personal preference
- Document decisions and maintain compliance-ready records

6. CROSS-FUNCTIONAL COLLABORATION STANDARDS

6.1 With COO

- Align on delivery platform technology, logistics systems, and operations technology impact
- Collaborate on market readiness technology evaluations

6.2 With CXO

- Monitor platform UX and app experience trajectory
- Evaluate technology decisions from customer experience and usability standpoint

6.3 With CFO

- Track infrastructure cost models, technology vendor spend, and platform scalability economics
- Align infrastructure investments with cost efficiency and scalability requirements

7. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

- Communicate concisely, with technology precision
- Avoid overly emotional, reactive, or impulsive language
- Provide executives with clear status updates and next steps
- Never communicate in a way that undermines trust or technology focus

8. REPORTING DISCIPLINE

- Deliver reports on schedule—no exceptions
- Ensure technology metrics and statements are fully supportable
- Do not revise technology data without documentation and CEO awareness

9. CONFIDENTIALITY REQUIREMENTS

- Protect all technology data, security materials, and internal technology reports
- Avoid discussing sensitive technology matters with unauthorized individuals
- Do not share technology data, screenshots, or documents outside approved channels

10. CONFLICT OF INTEREST & INDEPENDENCE

- Disclose any real or potential conflict to the CEO immediately
- The CTO must avoid financial entanglements with Crave'n vendors, partners, or competitors
- No use of company resources for personal benefit

11. MEETING CONDUCT STANDARDS

- Arrive prepared with all relevant technology metrics and documents
- Speak with clarity and technology-focused authority
- Do not dominate discussions—enable collaboration
- Keep debates professional, analytical, and respectful

12. DECISION-MAKING STANDARDS

- Base all recommendations on objective technology data
- Provide CEO with technology impact implications for each option
- Do not advance decisions without cross-functional consultation

13. CRISIS LEADERSHIP EXPECTATIONS

- Act with calm urgency—never panic
- Communicate quickly and accurately about technology impact
- Switch to crisis cadence: hourly/daily technology reporting
- Support CEO in stabilizing technology systems and security posture

14. TRAVEL & EXPENSE RESPONSIBILITIES

- Expenses must be reasonable, justified, and pre-approved when required
- No personal spending or mixed-use charges
- All receipts must be submitted timely
- Travel must be cost-conscious and aligned with corporate guidelines

15. TECHNOLOGY DOCUMENTATION STANDARDS

- Maintain complete, version-controlled technology documentation
- Ensure all technology changes are archived and timestamped
- Use compliance-ready structure for all technology data handling and security records

16. INTEGRITY IN TECHNOLOGY FORECASTING

- Technology projections must be realistic—not optimistic guesses
- Assumptions must be documented and shared
- CEO must be informed of any shift in technology forecast assumptions immediately

17. EXECUTIVE AVAILABILITY & RESPONSIVENESS

- Respond rapidly to CEO communications
- Be available during critical technology periods without exception
- Maintain predictable and reliable communication

18. RESPECT & CONDUCT WITHIN THE COMPANY

- No disrespectful or dismissive behavior
- No condescending language toward staff, executives, or engineering teams
- Uphold Crave'n's standards of professionalism and technology-first character

19. NON-FRATERNIZATION POLICY

The CTO must not engage in any romantic, financial, or inappropriate personal relationships with employees, executives, or contractors. This is to prevent conflicts of interest, favoritism, and organizational risk.

20. PERFORMANCE EVALUATION

- Quarterly performance review with CEO
- Annual performance evaluation
- Measurement based on system performance, security, leadership, and integrity

21. CEO & EXECUTIVE FEEDBACK LOOP

- The CTO must be open to constructive feedback
- Act promptly on CEO direction
- Demonstrate ongoing improvement in leadership, communication, and technology-focused discipline

22. VIOLATIONS & CONSEQUENCES

- Failure to meet reporting deadlines may result in corrective action
- Misrepresentation of technology data or security violations results in immediate termination
- Unprofessional behavior may result in disciplinary review
- Non-compliance with ethical standards or security requirements may trigger removal from role
