1. Purpose Of This Document

This Compliance & Regulatory Schedule outlines the entire customer data privacy, marketing compliance, and regulatory obligations that the Chief Experience Officer must maintain. This schedule follows Fortune 500 customer experience operations standards and ensures Crave'n remains compliant, audit-ready, and investor-ready at all times.

2. Core Compliance Domains

The CXO is responsible for compliance across these domains:

  • Customer data privacy compliance (GDPR, CCPA)
  • Marketing compliance and regulatory obligations
  • Customer communication audit requirements
  • Brand and messaging compliance
  • Customer data retention and deletion policies
  • Regulatory filings for customer data handling
  • Customer-facing system compliance
  • Vendor customer data handling compliance
  • Internal controls and audit readiness for customer data

3. Compliance Calendar Overview

This section defines all required compliance deliverables by frequency.

4. Monthly Compliance Obligations

  • Customer data privacy compliance review
  • Marketing compliance checkpoint
  • Monthly customer communication audit
  • Monthly customer data retention & documentation archive
  • Customer data handling vendor compliance updates
  • Compliance checklist confirmation to CEO

5. Quarterly Compliance Obligations

  • GDPR compliance review (if applicable)
  • CCPA compliance review (if applicable)
  • Marketing regulatory filings
  • Quarterly customer experience compliance review
  • Update customer experience risk register
  • Quarterly board-level customer experience packet (if applicable)

6. Annual Compliance Obligations

  • Annual customer data privacy compliance certification
  • Annual marketing compliance review
  • Annual report filing for customer data handling (if applicable)
  • Year-end customer experience statements
  • Annual compliance certification to CEO
  • Year-end customer data archive & system integrity audit

7. Event-Driven Compliance Obligations

  • Entering new states/markets with different privacy regulations
  • Signing material customer data handling vendor agreements
  • Customer data breach incidents
  • Changes in customer data privacy regulations
  • Marketing campaign launches requiring regulatory review

8. Documentation Retention Rules

  • Customer data privacy records: 7 years minimum
  • Marketing compliance records: 7 years minimum
  • Customer communication audit logs: 7 years minimum
  • Customer data handling vendor agreements: Duration + 7 years
  • Brand and messaging compliance documents: Permanent

9. Escalation Requirements

The CXO must immediately escalate to the CEO if:

  • A compliance deadline will be missed
  • A customer data privacy breach occurs
  • A compliance risk is identified
  • Regulatory correspondence is received

10. Compliance Reporting To CEO

A monthly compliance summary must be delivered that includes:

  • Upcoming deadlines
  • Completed obligations
  • Risks and flags
  • Escalations
  • Documentation status
