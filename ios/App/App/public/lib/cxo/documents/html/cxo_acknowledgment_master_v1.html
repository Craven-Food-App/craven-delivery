1. Purpose Of This Acknowledgment

This document certifies that the Chief Experience Officer of Crave'n has received, reviewed, and fully understands all policies, governance documents, expectations, and operating frameworks that define the CXO role within the organization.

Signing this document confirms the CXO's agreement to operate within the standards, responsibilities, compliance requirements, and reporting structures mandated by the CEO.

3. CXO Obligations

  • Operate within all listed policies and frameworks.
  • Meet all reporting deadlines.

  • Follow escalation procedures without delay.
  • Maintain confidentiality of company and customer information.
  • Adhere to all compliance and regulatory duties.
  • Honor decision-making boundaries defined by the CEO.
  • Notify the CEO of any conflicts of interest.
