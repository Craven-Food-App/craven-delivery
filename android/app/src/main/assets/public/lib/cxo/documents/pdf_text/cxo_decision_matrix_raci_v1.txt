4. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

CXO must escalate to CEO when:

- Customer satisfaction or retention changes materially

- Customer data privacy risk emerges

- Reputation or brand risk identified

- A decision impacts multiple departments

CXO holds independent authority over:

- Customer communication schedules

- Customer feedback collection

- Brand messaging guidelines

- Customer data privacy controls

- Customer experience measurement
