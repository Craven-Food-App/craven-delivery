1. Role Purpose And Strategic Mandate

The Chief Technology Officer of Crave'n is the executive leader responsible for technology strategy, platform architecture, engineering excellence, and infrastructure operations. The CTO ensures Crave'n operates with clear visibility into system performance, security posture, and technology capabilities while enabling the company to scale into a market-leading technology-enabled logistics and delivery platform.

The CTO partners with the CEO and executive team to translate Crave'n's mission into a scalable, secure, and reliable technology platform that drives sustainable growth through superior engineering and infrastructure delivery.

2. Strategic Accountability Domains

The CTO is accountable for the enterprise across the following core domains:

2.1 Technology Strategy And Planning

  • Develops and maintains the multi-year technology roadmap for Crave'n, including platform architecture, infrastructure scaling, and engineering capabilities.
  • Translates strategic objectives into quantifiable technology metrics, guardrails, and success criteria.
  • Supports market expansion, product decisions, and operational scaling with technology readiness analysis.

2.2 Platform Architecture And Engineering Excellence

  • Owns the design of platform architecture, system design, and engineering standards across all technology systems.
  • Ensures that all technology systems deliver consistent, high-quality, scalable, and secure experiences.
  • Implements disciplines that allow Crave'n to build and maintain market-leading technology capabilities.

2.3 Technology Performance And Reporting

  • Delivers clear, concise, and reliable technology metrics and performance reports to the CEO and executive team on a defined cadence.
  • Defines and maintains the core performance metrics for technology including uptime, latency, security posture, and engineering velocity.
  • Ensures stakeholders have decision-ready information about technology health and capabilities.

2.4 Infrastructure And Operations

  • Maintains a forward-looking view of infrastructure costs, scalability, and operational efficiency.
  • Monitors system performance and provides an always current view of technology health under multiple scenarios.
  • Establishes DevOps protocols, security standards, and infrastructure strategies consistent with growth plans.

2.5 Security And Compliance

  • Keeps Crave'n's technology secure and compliant with industry standards through well-researched security models.
  • Partners with the CEO and CFO on security strategy, infrastructure investments, and technology vendor analysis.
  • Coordinates security research, threat monitoring, and incident response during strategic planning.

2.6 Technology Data Governance

  • Ensures adherence to applicable technology data governance regulations and security standards for all relevant jurisdictions.
  • Implements technology risk monitoring that surfaces exposure early and clearly.
  • Supports governance processes, including system access controls and technology compliance.

2.7 Cross Functional Technology Integration

  • Partners with the COO on delivery platform technology, driver app systems, and logistics technology impact.
  • Partners with the CXO on customer-facing platform UX, app experience, and technology-enabled customer touchpoints.
  • Partners with the CFO on infrastructure costs, technology vendor oversight, and platform scalability economics.

4. Operating Cadence

The CTO is responsible for establishing and maintaining a reliable operating cadence that aligns with Crave'n's growth, technology expectations, and market leadership goals.

4.1 Daily

  • Review system performance, uptime, and security alerts from previous day.
  • Monitor infrastructure costs and technology trends against expectations.
  • Confirm that all technology systems are operational and delivering quality performance.
  • Escalate any material technology concern to the CEO without delay.

4.2 Weekly

  • Deliver a concise weekly technology snapshot to the CEO including uptime, latency, security posture, and infrastructure costs.
  • Highlight any deviations from plan or emerging technology risks.
  • Align with COO, CXO, and CFO on any technology impacts related to their domains.

4.3 Monthly

  • Produce complete monthly technology reports and a performance narrative for the CEO.
  • Update the technology roadmap and adjust for new information.
  • Conduct variance analysis versus targets and outline corrective actions where needed.
  • Review compliance status and upcoming technology security or regulatory obligations.

4.4 Quarterly

  • Conduct a structured technology review with the CEO and executive team.
  • Refine infrastructure, security, and platform architecture in partnership with CFO and COO.
  • Align platform UX and customer touchpoint roadmap with the CXO.
  • Prepare and maintain market-ready technology materials and narrative.

5. Cross Functional Collaboration Model

The CTO is expected to operate as a peer level partner with other executives and to provide technology leadership that enables, rather than obstructs, execution.

5.1 CEO Partnership

  • Provide the CEO with a continuous and unfiltered view of the company's technology position.
  • Support strategic decisions with technology impact assessments, tradeoffs, and risk evaluations.
  • Challenge assumptions constructively when technology risk is material.

5.2 COO Partnership

  • Work with the COO on delivery platform technology, driver app systems, and logistics technology integration.
  • Support logistics design with data on technology performance per order, per market, and per delivery type.

5.3 CXO Partnership

  • Evaluate platform UX and app experience relative to customer expectations and industry standards.
  • Participate in build versus buy assessments for customer-facing technology capabilities.

5.4 CFO Partnership

  • Collaborate on infrastructure cost models, technology vendor spend, and platform scalability economics.
  • Support technology investments with infrastructure cost and scalability analysis.

6. Governance, Controls, And Technology Integrity

The CTO is the primary steward of Crave'n's technology integrity and is expected to operate to a standard that would be acceptable in a market-leading technology company.

6.1 Integrity Standards

  • Technology reporting must be accurate, supportable, and free from intentional bias.
  • System access and data handling must be documented and consistently applied per security standards.
  • There must be no tolerance for security vulnerabilities, system failures, or undisclosed technology risks.

6.2 Control Environment

  • Design and maintain appropriate system access controls and security safeguards.
  • Implement approval workflows over technology changes and infrastructure modifications.
  • Ensure documentation is sufficient for security review, audit, or technology inquiries.

6.3 Escalation Expectations

  • Immediate escalation to the CEO for material technology incidents or security risk.
  • Immediate escalation for suspected security breaches, data violations, or compliance failures.
  • Timely escalation for upcoming security deadlines or potential non-compliance in technology systems.

7. Performance Measurement

The CTO's performance will be evaluated against qualitative and quantitative criteria that reflect the expectations of a Fortune 500 caliber technology leader in a scaling technology company.

7.1 Technology Outcomes

  • Accuracy and predictability of system performance and uptime metrics.
  • Strength and clarity of infrastructure cost and scalability management.
  • Absence of avoidable technology surprises or security incidents.

7.2 Operational Effectiveness

  • Reliability and timeliness of technology reporting cadence.
  • Stability and usability of technology systems and platforms.
  • Effectiveness of engineering processes and infrastructure operations.

7.3 Strategic Impact

  • Quality of technology insight in strategic decision making.
  • Support for market expansion and competitive positioning.
  • Contribution to building a scalable and resilient technology model.

7.4 Leadership And Culture

  • Ability to operate with calm, clarity, and technology-focused focus under pressure.
  • Quality of communication with the CEO, executives, and engineering teams.
  • Upholding a culture of engineering excellence, transparency, and accountability within and beyond the technology function.

8. Authority Statement

The CTO holds executive authority over technology operations, platform architecture, system security, and technology risk management, within the strategic parameters established by the CEO and the Board or equivalent governing body. All Crave'n personnel are expected to comply with the frameworks and policies established by the CTO and CEO in relation to technology.
