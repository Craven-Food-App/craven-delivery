## 1. Role Purpose And Strategic Mandate

The Chief Technology Officer of Crave'n is the executive leader responsible for technology strategy, platform architecture, engineering excellence, and infrastructure operations. The CTO ensures Crave'n operates with clear visibility into system performance, security posture, and technology capabilities while enabling the company to scale into a market-leading technology-enabled logistics and delivery platform.

The CTO partners with the CEO and executive team to translate Crave'n's mission into a scalable, secure, and reliable technology platform that drives sustainable growth through superior engineering and infrastructure delivery.

---

## 2. Strategic Accountability Domains

### 2.1 Technology Strategy And Planning

- Develops and maintains the multi-year technology roadmap
- Translates strategic objectives into quantifiable technology metrics
- Supports market expansion, product decisions, and operational scaling with technology readiness analysis

### 2.2 Platform Architecture And Engineering Excellence

- Owns the design of platform architecture, system design, and engineering standards
- Ensures that all technology systems deliver consistent, high-quality, scalable, and secure experiences
- Implements disciplines that allow Crave'n to build and maintain market-leading technology capabilities

### 2.3 Technology Performance And Reporting

- Delivers clear, concise, and reliable technology metrics and performance reports
- Defines and maintains core performance metrics: uptime, latency, security posture, engineering velocity
- Ensures stakeholders have decision-ready information about technology health and capabilities

### 2.4 Infrastructure And Operations

- Maintains forward-looking view of infrastructure costs, scalability, and operational efficiency
- Monitors system performance and provides current view of technology health
- Establishes DevOps protocols, security standards, and infrastructure strategies

### 2.5 Security And Compliance

- Keeps Crave'n's technology secure and compliant with industry standards
- Partners with CEO and CFO on security strategy, infrastructure investments, and technology vendor analysis
- Coordinates security research, threat monitoring, and incident response

### 2.6 Technology Data Governance

- Ensures adherence to technology data governance regulations and security standards
- Implements technology risk monitoring
- Supports governance processes for system access controls and technology compliance

### 2.7 Cross Functional Technology Integration

- Partners with COO on delivery platform technology, driver app systems, logistics technology impact
- Partners with CXO on customer-facing platform UX, app experience, technology-enabled customer touchpoints
- Partners with CFO on infrastructure costs, technology vendor oversight, platform scalability economics

## 4. Operating Cadence

### 4.1 Daily

- Review system performance, uptime, and security alerts
- Monitor infrastructure costs and technology trends
- Confirm all technology systems are operational
- Escalate material technology concerns to CEO

### 4.2 Weekly

- Deliver weekly technology snapshot to CEO (uptime, latency, security, infrastructure costs)
- Highlight deviations from plan or emerging technology risks
- Align with COO, CXO, and CFO on technology impacts

### 4.3 Monthly

- Produce complete monthly technology reports
- Update technology roadmap
- Conduct variance analysis versus targets
- Review compliance status for technology security and regulatory obligations

### 4.4 Quarterly

- Conduct structured technology review with CEO and executive team
- Refine infrastructure, security, and platform architecture
- Align platform UX roadmap with CXO
- Prepare market-ready technology materials

---

## 5. Cross Functional Collaboration Model

### 5.1 CEO Partnership

- Provide continuous and unfiltered view of technology position
- Support strategic decisions with technology impact assessments
- Challenge assumptions when technology risk is material

### 5.2 COO Partnership

- Work on delivery platform technology, driver app systems, logistics technology integration
- Support logistics design with technology performance data per order, per market

### 5.3 CXO Partnership

- Evaluate platform UX and app experience relative to customer expectations
- Participate in build versus buy assessments for customer-facing technology

### 5.4 CFO Partnership

- Collaborate on infrastructure cost models, technology vendor spend, platform scalability economics
- Support technology investments with infrastructure cost and scalability analysis

---

## 6. Governance, Controls, And Technology Integrity

### 6.1 Integrity Standards

- Technology reporting must be accurate, supportable, and free from bias
- System access and data handling must be documented and consistently applied per security standards
- No tolerance for security vulnerabilities, system failures, or undisclosed technology risks

### 6.2 Control Environment

- Design and maintain system access controls and security safeguards
- Implement approval workflows over technology changes and infrastructure modifications
- Ensure documentation sufficient for security review, audit, or technology inquiries

### 6.3 Escalation Expectations

- Immediate escalation for material technology incidents or security risk
- Immediate escalation for security breaches, data violations, or compliance failures
- Timely escalation for security deadlines or potential non-compliance

---

## 7. Performance Measurement

### 7.1 Technology Outcomes

- Accuracy and predictability of system performance and uptime metrics
- Strength of infrastructure cost and scalability management
- Absence of avoidable technology surprises or security incidents

### 7.2 Operational Effectiveness

- Reliability and timeliness of technology reporting
- Stability and usability of technology systems
- Effectiveness of engineering processes and infrastructure operations

### 7.3 Strategic Impact

- Quality of technology insight in strategic decision making
- Support for market expansion and competitive positioning
- Contribution to scalable and resilient technology model

### 7.4 Leadership And Culture

- Ability to operate with technology-focused focus under pressure
- Quality of communication with CEO, executives, and engineering teams
- Upholding culture of engineering excellence and accountability

---

## 8. Authority Statement

The CTO holds executive authority over technology operations, platform architecture, system security, and technology risk management, within strategic parameters established by the CEO and Board.
