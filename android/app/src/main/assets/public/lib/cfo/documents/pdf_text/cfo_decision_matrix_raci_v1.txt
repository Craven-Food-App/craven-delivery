4. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

CFO must escalate to CEO when:

- Runway changes materially

- Liquidity risk emerges

- Compliance or control failure identified

- Vendor or contract risk identified

CFO holds independent authority over:

- Reporting cadence

- Internal controls

- Reconciliations

- System design

- Expense governance
