## 1. Purpose Of This Manual

This Operating Manual defines the day-to-day, week-to-week, and month-to-month responsibilities of the Chief Financial Officer at Crave'n. It outlines required activities, workflows, review points, escalation paths, and decision-making processes.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities Overview

The CFO is responsible for oversight of:

- Financial strategy and forecasting
- Cash and liquidity management
- Financial reporting cadence and performance analysis
- Accounting operations and internal controls
- Compliance, tax, and regulatory obligations
- Investor readiness and capital strategy
- Cross-functional financial alignment
- Financial systems, accuracy, and data integrity

---

## 3. Daily Responsibilities

### 3.1 Cash & Liquidity Review

- Verify previous day bank balances
- Confirm incoming and outgoing payments
- Check for anomalies or unplanned transactions
- Update daily liquidity dashboard
- Flag variances > 5% of expected movement

### 3.2 Expense Monitoring

- Review transactions > approval thresholds
- Identify duplicate, unexpected, or suspicious vendor activity
- Ensure coding accuracy to chart of accounts

### 3.3 Interdepartmental Check-In

- Respond to CEO rapid requests
- Coordinate with COO on operational cost shifts
- Coordinate with CTO on cloud/platform billing patterns
- Coordinate with CXO on marketing and acquisition spend

### 3.4 Financial System Integrity

- Verify system uptime for accounting, treasury, reporting
- Ensure data sync across finance systems is complete
- Identify any reconciliation issues immediately

---

## 4. Weekly Responsibilities

### 4.1 Weekly Finance Packet To CEO

Must include:

- Cash on hand
- Weekly burn rate
- 12-week rolling runway forecast
- Top vendor exposures
- Variance vs weekly budget
- Notes on anomalies, risks, and mitigations

### 4.2 Spend & Vendor Review

- Review vendor spend by category
- Identify contracts nearing renewal
- Evaluate vendor ROI
- Flag high-risk vendors

### 4.3 Cross-Functional Alignment Sync

- COO: delivery economics, labor costs, logistics efficiency
- CTO: cloud, infrastructure, platform cost trajectory
- CXO: CAC, incentives, promotions cost impact

---

## 5. Monthly Responsibilities

### 5.1 Month-End Close

- Finalize all transactions for the period
- Complete general ledger reconciliation
- Verify accuracy of accruals, deferrals, and adjustments
- Review bank reconciliations
- Lock accounting period following CEO review

### 5.2 Monthly Financial Statements

Must deliver:

- Income statement
- Balance sheet
- Cash flow statement
- Unit economics summary
- Variance vs budget & forecast
- Narrative commentary for the CEO

### 5.3 Budgetary Compliance

- Assess department-level spend
- Identify departures from budget
- Recommend corrective actions
- Provide cost discipline guidance

### 5.4 Compliance & Filings Checkpoint

- Tax obligations
- Regulatory filings
- Corporate obligations
- Payroll tax verification

---

## 6. Quarterly Responsibilities

### 6.1 Quarterly Strategic Finance Review

- Long-range financial model update
- Pricing & payout structure review
- Gross margin improvement opportunities
- Market expansion financial readiness

### 6.2 Investor Readiness

- Prepare investor packet
- Refresh KPI dashboards
- Validate audited financial posture
- Ensure data room readiness

### 6.3 Risk Assessment Review

- Operational risk exposure
- Liquidity risk
- Compliance risk
- Technology spend trajectory

---

## 7. Annual Responsibilities

- Create annual budget
- Formalize 3-year financial model
- Review vendor contracts & renegotiations
- Perform fraud risk assessment
- Re-evaluate financial systems architecture
- Prepare annual CEO financial strategy summary

---

## 8. Financial Controls Responsibilities

- Define segregation of duties
- Implement dual-approval thresholds
- Maintain access control lists
- Monitor data integrity
- Ensure audit-ready documentation
- Review all financial policies annually

---

## 9. Crisis Response Responsibilities

### 9.1 Immediate Action Protocol

- Freeze all non-essential spending
- Provide CEO with emergency financial summary
- Reforecast based on crisis scenario
- Escalate vendor and banking risks

### 9.2 Communication Protocol

- CEO receives hourly or daily updates as required
- Department heads receive impact analysis
- External stakeholders notified only with CEO direction

---

## 10. Executive Collaboration Processes

### 10.1 CFO + CEO

- Cash visibility
- Strategic decision financial assessment
- Capital & fundraising strategy

### 10.2 CFO + COO

- Driver payouts
- Logistics cost structure
- Market expansion readiness

### 10.3 CFO + CTO

- Infrastructure cost control
- Tech vendor oversight
- Platform scalability economics

### 10.4 CFO + CXO

- CAC & LTV tracking
- Incentive & promotion financial impact
- Customer and driver retention economics

---

## 11. Escalation Expectations

- Cash runway risks
- Compliance breaches
- Vendor or contract exposure
- Budget blowouts
- Miscalculated projections
- System failures impacting financial data
