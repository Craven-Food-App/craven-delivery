1. Purpose Of This Document

This Compliance & Regulatory Schedule outlines the entire financial, tax, payroll, and corporate compliance obligations that the Chief Financial Officer must maintain. This schedule follows the same standards used by Fortune 500 financial operations teams and ensures Crave'n remains compliant, audit-ready, and investor-ready at all times.

2. Core Compliance Domains

The CFO is responsible for compliance across these domains:

  • Federal tax compliance
  • State and local tax compliance
  • Payroll and employment tax compliance
  • Corporate governance filings
  • Financial reporting integrity
  • Regulatory filings for multi-state operations
  • Banking and treasury compliance
  • Vendor contract and W-9 compliance
  • Internal controls and audit readiness

3. Compliance Calendar Overview

This section defines all required compliance deliverables by frequency.

4. Monthly Compliance Obligations

  • Payroll tax filing & payment
  • State sales tax filing (if applicable)
  • Monthly financial statement close
  • Monthly reconciliation & documentation archive
  • Vendor 1099 tracking updates
  • Compliance checklist confirmation to CEO

5. Quarterly Compliance Obligations

  • Federal 941 payroll tax filing
  • State withholding tax filings
  • State unemployment filings
  • Quarterly financial reporting compliance review
  • Update financial risk register
  • Quarterly board-level finance packet (if applicable)
  • Quarterly 409A review (if equity grants active)

6. Annual Compliance Obligations

  • Federal corporate tax return (Form 1120 or applicable)
  • State corporate tax returns
  • Annual report filing for each incorporated state
  • Registered agent renewal
  • Year-end financial statements
  • W-2 issuance to employees
  • 1099 issuance to contractors & vendors
  • Annual compliance certification to CEO
  • Year-end financial archive & system integrity audit

7. Event-Driven Compliance Obligations

  • Formation of new subsidiaries/entities
  • Entering new states/markets for business operations
  • Signing material vendor agreements
  • Bank account openings or modifications
  • Fundraising events (SAFE, equity, debt, etc.)
  • Equity grant issuance (triggering 409A updates)
  • Changes in executive leadership

8. Documentation Retention Rules

  • Financial statements: 7 years minimum
  • Tax returns: 7 years minimum
  • General ledger: Permanent
  • Payroll records: 7 years minimum
  • Vendor agreements: Duration + 7 years
  • Corporate governance documents: Permanent

9. Escalation Requirements

The CFO must immediately escalate to the CEO if:

  • A tax filing deadline will be missed
  • A statutory payment will be late
  • A compliance risk is identified
  • State registration issues occur
  • Any regulatory correspondence is received

10. Compliance Reporting To CEO

A monthly compliance summary must be delivered that includes:

  • Upcoming deadlines
  • Completed obligations
  • Risks and flags
  • Escalations
  • Documentation status
