1. ROLE PURPOSE AND STRATEGIC MANDATE

The Chief Financial Officer of Crave'n is the executive leader responsible for financial strategy, financial integrity, and disciplined execution across the enterprise. The CFO ensures Crave'n operates with clear visibility into performance, risk, and runway while enabling the company to scale into a high growth technology enabled logistics and delivery platform.

The CFO partners with the CEO and executive team to translate Crave'n's mission into a financially sustainable and investable business model. The CFO is accountable for building and maintaining a finance environment that meets or exceeds the expectations of sophisticated investors, strategic partners, and future regulatory stakeholders.

2. STRATEGIC ACCOUNTABILITY DOMAINS

The CFO is accountable for the enterprise across the following core domains.

2.1 Financial Strategy And Planning

- Develops and maintains the multi year financial model for Crave'n, including revenue, gross margin, operating expense, capital needs, and cash flow.

- Translates strategic objectives into quantifiable financial targets, guardrails, and tradeoffs.

- Supports market expansion, pricing, payout, and product decisions with scenario based analysis.

2.2 Financial Operations And Controls

- Owns the design of the chart of accounts, transaction flows, and reconciliation processes.

- Ensures that all financial systems are accurate, timely, and appropriately controlled.

- Implements disciplines that allow Crave'n to withstand diligence, audits, and investor scrutiny.

2.3 Business Performance And Reporting

- Delivers clear, concise, and reliable financial and operating reports to the CEO and executive team on a defined cadence.

- Defines and maintains the core performance metrics for the business including unit economics, gross margin, contribution margin, and runway.

- Ensures stakeholders have decision ready information rather than raw data.

2.4 Liquidity, Cash Management, And Runway

- Maintains a forward looking view of the company's cash position and obligations.

- Monitors burn rate and provides an always current view of runway under multiple scenarios.

- Establishes cash handling protocols, reserves policies, and banking relationships consistent with growth plans and risk appetite.

2.5 Investor Readiness And Capital Formation

- Keeps Crave'n in an investor ready state through well organized financials, cap table, and support materials.

- Partners with the CEO on capital strategy, valuation analysis, and use of proceeds modeling.

- Coordinates data rooms, financial schedules, and responses during investor diligence.

2.6 Compliance, Governance, And Risk Oversight

- Ensures adherence to applicable corporate, tax, and reporting requirements for all relevant jurisdictions.

- Implements financial risk monitoring that surfaces exposure early and clearly.

- Supports governance processes, including board reporting and formal corporate records related to finance.

2.7 Cross Functional Finance Integration

- Partners with the COO on operating cost structures, driver payouts, and logistics economics.

- Partners with the CTO on platform, infrastructure, and vendor spend and on build versus buy decisions.

- Partners with the CXO on pricing, promotions, incentives, and acquisition cost dynamics.

4. OPERATING CADENCE

The CFO is responsible for establishing and maintaining a reliable operating cadence that aligns with Crave'n's growth, investor expectations, and board level oversight.

4.1 Daily

- Review prior day cash flows, bank balances, and any unusual transactions.

- Monitor spend against expectations and identify emerging risks.

- Confirm that core finance systems are operational and updated.

- Escalate any material financial concern to the CEO without delay.

4.2 Weekly

- Deliver a concise weekly finance snapshot to the CEO including cash, burn rate, and 12 week runway.

- Highlight any deviations from plan or emerging cost or revenue patterns.

- Align with COO, CTO, and CXO on any financial impacts related to their domains.

4.3 Monthly

- Produce complete monthly financial statements and a performance narrative for the CEO.

- Update the rolling financial model and adjust for new information.

- Conduct variance analysis versus budget and outline corrective actions where needed.

- Review compliance status and upcoming regulatory or tax obligations.

4.4 Quarterly

- Conduct a structured business performance review with the CEO and executive team.

- Refine pricing, payout, and fee structures in partnership with CXO and COO.

- Align infrastructure and vendor cost roadmap with the CTO.

- Prepare and maintain investor ready financial materials and narrative.

5. CROSS FUNCTIONAL COLLABORATION MODEL

The CFO is expected to operate as a peer level partner with other executives and to provide financial leadership that enables, rather than obstructs, execution.

5.1 CEO Partnership

- Provide the CEO with a continuous and unfiltered view of the company's financial position.

- Support strategic decisions with options, tradeoffs, and risk assessments.

- Challenge assumptions constructively when financial risk is material.

5.2 COO Partnership

- Work with the COO on driver payouts, delivery economics, and expansion costs by geography.

- Support logistics design with data on cost per order, per mile, and per market.

5.3 CTO Partnership

- Evaluate infrastructure and platform spend relative to growth and reliability requirements.

- Participate in build versus buy assessments for major technology or data capabilities.

5.4 CXO Partnership

- Collaborate on customer and driver acquisition cost, incentive structures, and retention economics.

- Support promotions and programs with profitability and payback period analysis.

6. GOVERNANCE, CONTROLS, AND FINANCIAL INTEGRITY

The CFO is the primary steward of Crave'n's financial integrity and is expected to operate to a standard that would be acceptable in a public company environment, even before any listing.

6.1 Integrity Standards

- Financial reporting must be accurate, supportable, and free from intentional bias.

- Adjustments, estimates, and judgments must be documented and consistently applied.

- There must be no tolerance for hidden liabilities, off book arrangements, or undisclosed commitments.

6.2 Control Environment

- Design and maintain appropriate segregation of duties within finance processes.

- Implement access controls over financial systems and data.

- Ensure documentation is sufficient for independent review, diligence, or audit.

6.3 Escalation Expectations

- Immediate escalation to the CEO for material deviations from projected runway or covenant risk.

- Immediate escalation for suspected fraud, financial misconduct, or material control failures.

- Timely escalation for upcoming regulatory deadlines or potential non compliance.

7. PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT

The CFO's performance will be evaluated against qualitative and quantitative criteria that reflect the expectations of a Fortune 500 caliber financial leader in a scaling technology company.

7.1 Financial Outcomes

- Accuracy and predictability of financial results and forecasts.

- Strength and clarity of cash and runway management.

- Absence of avoidable financial surprises.

7.2 Operational Effectiveness

- Reliability and timeliness of reporting cadence.

- Stability and usability of financial systems.

- Effectiveness of cost management and resource allocation support.

7.3 Strategic Impact

- Quality of financial insight in strategic decision making.

- Support for fundraising and investor engagement.

- Contribution to building a scalable and resilient business model.

7.4 Leadership And Culture

- Ability to operate with calm, clarity, and discipline under pressure.

- Quality of communication with the CEO, executives, and stakeholders.

- Upholding a culture of integrity, transparency, and accountability within and beyond the finance function.

8. AUTHORITY STATEMENT

The CFO holds executive authority over financial operations, financial systems, financial reporting, and financial risk management, within the strategic parameters established by the CEO and the Board or equivalent governing body. All Crave'n personnel are expected to comply with the frameworks and policies established by the CFO and CEO in relation to finance.

9. EFFECTIVE DATE AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
