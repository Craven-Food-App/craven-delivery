## 1. Purpose Of This Manual

This Operating Manual defines the day-to-day, week-to-week, and month-to-month responsibilities of the Chief Technology Officer at Crave'n. It outlines required activities, workflows, review points, escalation paths, and decision-making processes.

---

## 2. Core Responsibilities Overview

The CTO is responsible for oversight of:

- Technology strategy and architecture planning
- System performance and reliability management
- Technology metrics reporting and performance analysis
- Security, compliance, and data governance
- Engineering standards and code quality
- Infrastructure cost and scalability strategy
- Cross-functional technology alignment
- Technology systems, accuracy, and data integrity

---

## 3. Daily Responsibilities

### 3.1 System Performance Review

- Review previous day system uptime, latency, and performance metrics
- Identify trends, issues, or opportunities requiring immediate attention
- Flag critical technology concerns for escalation
- Update daily technology health dashboard

### 3.2 Security Monitoring

- Review security alerts and vulnerability reports
- Monitor infrastructure costs and technology trends
- Ensure security standards are maintained across all systems

### 3.3 Interdepartmental Check-In

- Respond to CEO rapid requests
- Coordinate with COO on delivery platform technology and logistics systems
- Coordinate with CXO on platform UX and app experience issues
- Coordinate with CFO on infrastructure costs and technology vendor impacts

### 3.4 Technology System Integrity

- Verify system uptime for all technology platforms
- Ensure technology systems are delivering quality performance
- Identify any technology issues immediately

---

## 4. Weekly Responsibilities

### 4.1 Weekly Technology Packet To CEO

Must include:

- System uptime and performance metrics
- Security posture and vulnerability status
- Infrastructure cost trends
- Top technology risks and issues
- Variance vs weekly targets
- Notes on technology risks and mitigations

### 4.2 Technology Architecture Review

- Review technology architecture by system and component
- Identify scalability or performance improvement opportunities
- Evaluate technology vendor and tool effectiveness

### 4.3 Cross-Functional Alignment Sync

- COO: delivery platform technology, driver app systems, logistics technology impact
- CXO: platform UX, app experience, technology-enabled customer touchpoints
- CFO: infrastructure cost models, technology vendor spend, platform scalability economics

---

## 5. Monthly Responsibilities

### 5.1 Month-End Technology Review

- Finalize all technology metrics for the period
- Complete technology architecture analysis
- Verify accuracy of security and compliance data
- Review technology change audit trail

### 5.2 Monthly Technology Reports

Must deliver:

- System performance summary (uptime, latency, reliability)
- Security posture analysis
- Infrastructure cost and scalability report
- Engineering velocity and code quality metrics
- Variance vs targets & forecast
- Narrative commentary for the CEO

### 5.3 Security And Compliance Review

- Assess security posture across systems
- Identify security vulnerabilities or compliance gaps
- Recommend corrective actions
- Provide security discipline guidance

### 5.4 Infrastructure Cost Checkpoint

- Infrastructure cost obligations
- Technology vendor contract reviews
- Infrastructure optimization opportunities

---

## 6. Quarterly Responsibilities

### 6.1 Quarterly Strategic Technology Review

- Long-range technology roadmap update
- Infrastructure cost and scalability structure review
- Technology vendor and tool improvement opportunities
- Market expansion technology readiness

### 6.2 Technology Readiness

- Prepare technology positioning materials
- Refresh technology KPI dashboards
- Validate security and compliance posture
- Ensure technology research readiness

### 6.3 Risk Assessment Review

- System performance risk exposure
- Security vulnerability risk
- Infrastructure cost risk
- Technology vendor risk trajectory

---

## 7. Annual Responsibilities

- Create annual technology plan
- Formalize 3-year technology vision
- Review engineering standards & code quality guidelines
- Perform security and compliance risk assessment
- Re-evaluate technology systems architecture
- Prepare annual CEO technology strategy summary

---

## 8. Technology Controls Responsibilities

- Define system access and security controls
- Implement approval workflows for technology changes
- Maintain engineering standard enforcement
- Monitor technology data integrity
- Ensure compliance-ready documentation
- Review all technology policies annually

---

## 9. Crisis Response Responsibilities

### 9.1 Immediate Action Protocol

- Freeze all non-critical technology changes if security risk
- Provide CEO with emergency technology summary
- Reforecast based on crisis scenario
- Escalate security and infrastructure risks

### 9.2 Communication Protocol

- CEO receives hourly or daily updates as required
- Department heads receive technology impact analysis
- External stakeholders notified only with CEO direction

---

## 10. Executive Collaboration Processes

### 10.1 CTO + CEO

- Technology visibility
- Strategic decision technology impact assessment
- Technology investment & vendor strategy

### 10.2 CTO + COO

- Delivery platform technology optimization
- Driver app system standards
- Logistics technology integration

### 10.3 CTO + CXO

- Platform UX and app experience control
- Customer-facing technology oversight
- Customer touchpoint scalability economics

### 10.4 CTO + CFO

- Infrastructure cost tracking
- Technology vendor financial impact
- Platform scalability economics

---

## 11. Escalation Expectations

- System downtime or performance risks
- Security breaches
- Infrastructure cost exposure
- Technology vendor risks
- Miscalculated technology projections
- System failures impacting operations
