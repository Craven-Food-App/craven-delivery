1. Purpose Of This Matrix

This Decision Rights Matrix defines the formal decision-making authority and responsibilities for Crave'n's Chief Technology Officer (CTO), in relation to the CEO, CFO, COO, and CXO. It establishes clear accountability and alignment across strategic, technology, security, and infrastructure-related areas.

2. RACI Legend

  • D – Decision Authority: Final decision-making power.
  • A – Accountable: Owns the outcome; answerable for final output.
  • R – Responsible: Performs the work or analysis.
  • C – Consulted: Provides input before a decision.
  • I – Informed: Notified after a decision.

4. Rules Of Engagement

4.1 CTO Must Escalate To CEO When:

  • System downtime or performance degradation deviates materially from targets.
  • Security breach or vulnerability risk surfaces.
  • Infrastructure cost exposure appears.
  • A decision has cross-functional technology impact.

4.2 CTO Has Independent Authority Over:

  • Engineering standards and code quality.
  • System architecture and design patterns.
  • Security controls and vulnerability management.
  • Technology tool and framework selection.
  • Infrastructure provisioning and scaling.

5. Revision & Review

This matrix shall be reviewed annually by the CTO and CEO. Updates require CEO approval.
