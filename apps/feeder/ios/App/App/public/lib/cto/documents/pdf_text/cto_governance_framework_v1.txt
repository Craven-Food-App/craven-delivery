1. PURPOSE OF THIS FRAMEWORK

This framework defines how the CTO establishes, operates, and maintains technology governance at Crave'n. It ensures engineering excellence, security, scalability, and alignment across all technology domains. Designed to match Fortune 500 tech-company standards.

2. GOVERNANCE SCOPE

The CTO's governance authority includes:

- Technology strategy & planning
- Platform infrastructure & operations
- Engineering standards & code quality
- Security, compliance, & data governance
- System performance & reliability
- Technology vendor & tool selection
- Cross-functional technology planning
- Technology systems & data governance

3. GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

Engineering Excellence – Technology systems must be reliable, scalable, secure, and maintainable.

Security First – Security must be built into all technology decisions from the start.

Scalability & Performance – Infrastructure standards, performance requirements, and scalability guardrails are enforced.

Alignment & Integration – Technology aligns with operational and business realities.

Innovation & Efficiency – Technology enables innovation while maintaining cost efficiency.

4. GOVERNANCE OPERATING MODEL

Strategic Governance:

- Annual technology plan
- Strategic technology vision
- Technology investment strategy
- Platform architecture governance

Operational Governance:

- Technology reporting cadence
- System performance monitoring
- Infrastructure cost management
- Delegation of authority

Control Governance:

- Security controls
- System access governance
- Compliance monitoring
- Risk escalation pathways

Information Governance:

- Technology systems ownership
- Engineering standards
- Data retention & audit readiness
- Technology data source-of-truth architecture

5. DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY AND ESCALATION

Escalation triggers include:

- System downtime or performance degradation
- Security breaches or vulnerabilities
- Compliance risk
- Technology failures
- Any threat to operations or business continuity

Escalation path:

1. CTO to CEO
2. CTO to COO/CFO/CXO (if impacted)
3. CTO to Board (through CEO)

6. GOVERNANCE CADENCE

Weekly:

- Technology snapshot
- System performance update
- Security alert review

Monthly:

- Full technology reporting
- Variance analysis
- Compliance checkpoint

Quarterly:

- Strategic review
- Infrastructure cost review
- Technology roadmap update

7. GOVERNANCE OUTPUTS

- Annual technology plan
- Weekly technology summary
- Monthly reporting package
- Quarterly strategic review
- Security schedule
- Technology risk register
- Delegation of authority matrix

8. GOVERNANCE RISK DOMAINS

- System uptime & performance risk
- Security vulnerability risk
- Infrastructure cost exposure
- Technology vendor risk
- Compliance & regulatory risk
- Engineering velocity risk
