1. PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

This Reporting Cadence outlines the exact frequency, structure, and expectations for all technology and system performance reporting performed by the Chief Technology Officer. It establishes a consistent, reliable rhythm that mirrors Fortune 500 technology operating standards.

2. REPORTING CADENCE OVERVIEW

The CTO must deliver accurate, timely, and decision-ready technology insights across:

- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Annual
- Ad-Hoc (CEO-requested)

3. DAILY REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Daily System Health Status

- System uptime and availability
- Performance metrics (latency, throughput)
- Security alerts and vulnerability status
- Variance vs technology forecast

3.2 Technology System Health

- Infrastructure sync verification
- System integrity status
- Any anomalies requiring escalation

3.3 Daily Notes To CEO (If Needed)

- Unexpected system incidents
- Security concerns
- Urgent technology risks or concerns

4. WEEKLY REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

Weekly reports must be delivered every Friday by 3:00 PM ET.

4.1 Weekly Technology Packet To CEO

- System uptime and performance
- Security posture and vulnerability status
- Infrastructure cost trends
- Top technology risks and issues
- Variance to weekly targets
- Operational notes and flags
- Action items for next week

4.2 Technology Architecture Summary

- COO: delivery platform technology, driver app systems, logistics technology impact
- CXO: platform UX, app experience, technology-enabled customer touchpoints
- CFO: infrastructure cost models, technology vendor spend, platform scalability economics

4.3 Security And Performance Review

- Security posture across systems
- System performance effectiveness
- Infrastructure optimization opportunities

5. MONTHLY REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

Must be delivered no later than the 5th business day following month-end.

5.1 Monthly Technology Statements

- System Performance Summary (uptime, latency, reliability)
- Security Posture Analysis
- Infrastructure Cost and Scalability Report
- Engineering Velocity and Code Quality Metrics

5.2 Monthly Technology Metrics

- System performance by platform
- Security vulnerability analysis
- Infrastructure cost vs budget
- Engineering velocity and quality trends

5.3 Monthly Variance Report

- Target vs Actual
- Forecast vs Actual
- Major variance explanations
- Corrective actions

5.4 CEO Monthly Commentary

Executive summary narrative explaining technology trends, risks, opportunities, and directional recommendations.

6. QUARTERLY REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

Must be delivered by the 10th business day following quarter-end.

6.1 Quarterly Strategy Packet

- Updated 3-year technology roadmap
- Infrastructure cost and scalability structure analyses
- Technology vendor and tool assessment
- Security posture trend review
- Market expansion technology readiness

6.2 Quarterly KPI Dashboard

- System uptime and performance trends
- Security posture metrics
- Infrastructure cost per platform
- Engineering velocity trends

6.3 Quarterly Compliance Review

- Security compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Technology regulatory reporting
- System access audit status
- Risk and exposure updates

7. ANNUAL REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

- Annual technology plan and roadmap
- Formal 3-year long-range technology vision
- Year-end technology statements
- Annual infrastructure cost and scalability projection
- Year-end technology positioning and market readiness packet

8. AD-HOC REPORTING

The CEO may request special or urgent reporting on:

- Market expansion technology analysis
- Infrastructure cost and scalability sensitivity analysis
- Crisis technology modeling
- Security deep-dive review

9. REPORTING ACCURACY STANDARDS

- Numbers must reconcile to systems of record
- All assumptions must be documented
- Forecasts must have supporting schedules
- No rounding or simplification without disclosure
- No unverified technology data allowed

10. ESCALATION RULES

- Missed reporting deadlines → immediate CEO escalation
- Material variance → CEO must be notified same-day
- Data integrity issues → immediate root-cause analysis
- Unexpected system incidents → flag within 24 hours
