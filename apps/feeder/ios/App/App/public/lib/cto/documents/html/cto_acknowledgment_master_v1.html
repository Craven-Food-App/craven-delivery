1. Purpose Of This Acknowledgment

This document certifies that the Chief Technology Officer of Crave'n has received, reviewed, and fully understands all policies, governance documents, expectations, and operating frameworks that define the CTO role within the organization.

Signing this document confirms the CTO's agreement to operate within the standards, responsibilities, compliance requirements, and reporting structures mandated by the CEO.

2. Documents Covered Under This Acknowledgment

The CTO acknowledges receipt and understanding of the following:

  • CTO Charter & Role Definition
  • CTO Governance Framework
  • CTO Responsibilities Checklist
  • CTO Executive Handbook
  • CTO Operating Manual
  • CTO Decision Rights Matrix (RACI)
  • CTO Reporting Cadence
  • CTO Compliance & Regulatory Schedule
  • CTO System Access Governance & Data Ownership Map
  • All supplemental CEO directives and amendments

3. CTO Obligations

  • Operate within all listed policies and frameworks.
  • Meet all reporting deadlines.
  • Maintain ethical and professional conduct at all times.
  • Follow escalation procedures without delay.
  • Maintain confidentiality of company and technology information.
  • Adhere to all compliance and regulatory duties.
  • Honor decision-making boundaries defined by the CEO.
  • Notify the CEO of any conflicts of interest.
