## 1. Purpose Of This Matrix

Defines decision-making authority for CXO and cross-functional executives. Ensures alignment, accountability, and clarity at scale.

---

## 2. RACI Legend

**D – Decision Authority:** Final decision power  

**A – Accountable:** Owns the outcome  

**R – Responsible:** Performs the work  

**C – Consulted:** Provides input  

**I – Informed:** Updated afterward

## 4. Rules Of Engagement

### CXO Must Escalate To CEO When:

- Customer satisfaction or retention materially deviates  

- Customer data privacy breach or compliance risk emerges  

- Reputation incident or brand damage risk appears  

- A decision impacts multiple departments  

### CXO Independent Authority Includes:

- Customer communication schedules  

- Customer feedback collection  

- Brand messaging guidelines  

- Customer data privacy controls  

- Customer experience measurement
