1. Purpose Of This Framework

This Governance Framework defines how the Chief Experience Officer (CXO) establishes, operates, and maintains the customer experience governance environment for Crave'n. It provides enterprise-level guardrails that ensure customer-centricity, brand consistency, data privacy compliance, and alignment across all customer-facing domains.

This framework is designed to mirror the standards used in Fortune 500 technology and consumer-facing organizations, adapted to Crave'n's high-growth environment.

2. Governance Scope

The CXO's governance authority applies to the following domains:

  • Customer experience strategy, planning, and journey optimization
  • Brand identity, messaging, and customer communications
  • Customer metrics, reporting, and performance analytics
  • Customer data privacy, GDPR/CCPA compliance, and data governance
  • Marketing compliance and regulatory obligations
  • Customer acquisition and retention strategy
  • Cross-functional customer experience alignment with COO, CTO, CFO, and CEO
  • Customer-facing systems, tools, and internal customer data governance

3. Governance Principles

3.1 Customer-Centricity

All decisions must prioritize customer value and experience quality. The CXO ensures the CEO always receives a clear and honest view of customer sentiment and experience health.

3.2 Brand Consistency

Brand identity, messaging, and customer touchpoints must be reliable and consistent across all channels. Unexpected brand deviations are escalated immediately.

3.3 Data Privacy & Compliance

The CXO defines customer data handling workflows, privacy controls, and compliance requirements. The CXO enforces these controls across all customer-facing departments.

3.4 Alignment & Integration

All customer experience decisions must be aligned with operational and technical realities. The CXO collaborates closely with COO, CTO, CFO, and CEO.

3.5 Scalability

Customer experience systems must scale seamlessly as Crave'n expands into new markets and higher customer volumes.

4. Governance Operating Model

4.1 Strategic Governance

  • Annual customer experience plan and roadmap
  • 3-year customer experience strategic vision
  • Customer acquisition and retention strategy
  • Brand positioning and market engagement governance

4.2 Operational Governance

  • Weekly, monthly, and quarterly customer metrics reporting cadence
  • Customer feedback monitoring and response workflows
  • Customer communication and marketing campaign governance
  • Approval workflows and delegation of authority for customer-facing initiatives

4.3 Control Governance

  • Customer data privacy controls and GDPR/CCPA compliance
  • Customer data integrity and system access governance
  • Marketing compliance monitoring and regulatory obligations
  • Risk identification, escalation, and mitigation for customer experience

4.4 Information Governance

  • Ownership of customer data systems and customer datasets
  • Customer metrics standards and KPI definitions
  • Data retention and privacy compliance readiness
  • Source-of-truth customer data architecture

5. Delegation Of Authority & Escalation

The CXO must operate within the enterprise Delegation of Authority (DOA) framework approved by the CEO. The CXO may further delegate operational-level responsibilities, but accountability remains with the CXO at all times.

5.1 Escalation Triggers

  • Material deviation from customer satisfaction or retention targets
  • Customer data privacy breaches or compliance risks
  • Non-compliance with marketing regulations or customer data privacy laws
  • Reputation incidents or brand damage risks
  • Any customer experience risk that may impact operations or market position

5.2 Escalation Path

  1. CXO → CEO (immediate escalation)
  2. CXO → COO/CTO/CFO (cross-functional impact)
  3. CXO → Board or Advisors (CEO direction required)

6. Governance Cadence

6.1 Weekly

  • Customer experience snapshot to CEO
  • Customer metrics update (NPS, CSAT, retention)
  • Customer feedback review and response flags

6.2 Monthly

  • Full customer experience reporting package
  • Variance analysis and performance narrative
  • Compliance and regulatory checkpoint for customer data privacy

6.3 Quarterly

  • Strategic customer experience review
  • Pricing, promotions, and incentive structure review
  • Market readiness and competitive positioning update

7. Governance Outputs

  • Annual customer experience plan
  • Weekly customer experience summary
  • Monthly reporting packet
  • Quarterly strategic review packet
  • Compliance and privacy schedule
  • Customer experience risk register
  • Delegation of authority and approval matrix

8. Governance Risk Domains

  • Customer satisfaction and retention
  • Customer data privacy and compliance
  • Brand reputation and market position
  • Customer acquisition cost pressures
  • Marketing compliance and regulatory risk
  • Customer experience consistency across channels

9. Amendment And Review

This Governance Framework will be reviewed annually by the CXO and CEO, or more frequently if required based on business scale or regulatory requirements.
