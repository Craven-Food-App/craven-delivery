1. Purpose Of This Document

This Reporting Cadence outlines the exact frequency, structure, and expectations for all customer experience and brand reporting performed by the Chief Experience Officer. It establishes a consistent, reliable rhythm that mirrors Fortune 500 customer experience operating standards while supporting Crave'n's high-growth environment.

2. Reporting Cadence Overview

The CXO must deliver accurate, timely, and decision-ready customer experience insights across the following standardized reporting intervals:

  • Daily
  • Weekly
  • Monthly
  • Quarterly
  • Annual
  • Ad-Hoc (CEO-requested)

3. Daily Reporting Requirements

3.1 Daily Customer Health Status

  • Customer feedback summary
  • Support ticket volume and resolution
  • Customer satisfaction scores (NPS, CSAT)
  • Variance vs customer experience forecast

3.2 Customer-Facing System Health

  • Customer communication system sync verification
  • Customer data integrity status
  • Any anomalies requiring escalation

3.3 Daily Notes To CEO (If Needed)

  • Unexpected customer experience incidents
  • Brand reputation concerns
  • Urgent customer data privacy risks or concerns

4. Weekly Reporting Requirements

Weekly reporting is the foundation of Crave'n's customer experience visibility. Unless otherwise noted, all weekly reports must be delivered every Friday by 3:00 PM ET.

4.1 Weekly Customer Experience Packet To CEO

  • NPS and CSAT scores
  • Customer retention rate
  • Customer acquisition cost trends
  • Top customer feedback themes
  • Variance to weekly targets
  • Operational notes and flags
  • Action items for next week

4.2 Customer Journey Summary

  • COO: delivery experience, driver interactions, logistics customer impact
  • CTO: platform UX, app experience, technology-enabled customer touchpoints
  • CFO: pricing models, promotional spend, acquisition cost economics

4.3 Brand And Communication Review

  • Brand consistency across channels
  • Customer communication effectiveness
  • Marketing campaign performance

5. Monthly Reporting Requirements

Monthly reporting must be delivered no later than the 5th business day following month-end. CXO must escalate delays to the CEO immediately.

5.1 Monthly Customer Experience Statements

  • Customer Satisfaction Summary (NPS, CSAT)
  • Customer Retention Analysis
  • Customer Acquisition Cost and Lifetime Value Report
  • Customer Journey Performance by Touchpoint

5.2 Monthly Customer Metrics

  • Customer satisfaction by market
  • Customer retention analysis
  • Customer acquisition vs retention cost
  • Brand perception and reputation metrics

5.3 Monthly Variance Report

  • Target vs Actual
  • Forecast vs Actual
  • Major variance explanations
  • Corrective actions

5.4 CEO Monthly Commentary

The CXO must produce an executive summary narrative that explains customer experience trends, risks, opportunities, and directional recommendations.

6. Quarterly Reporting Requirements

Quarterly reporting forms the basis of strategic alignment and must be delivered by the 10th business day following quarter-end.

6.1 Quarterly Strategy Packet

  • Updated 3-year customer experience roadmap
  • Pricing and promotional structure analyses
  • Customer retention strategy assessment
  • Brand positioning trend review
  • Market expansion customer experience readiness

6.2 Quarterly KPI Dashboard

  • NPS and CSAT trends
  • Customer retention rate
  • Customer acquisition cost per market
  • Customer lifetime value trends

6.3 Quarterly Compliance Review

  • Customer data privacy compliance (GDPR, CCPA)
  • Marketing compliance and regulatory reporting
  • Customer communication audit status
  • Risk and exposure updates

7. Annual Reporting Requirements

  • Annual customer experience plan and roadmap
  • Formal 3-year long-range customer experience vision
  • Year-end customer experience statements
  • Annual customer acquisition and retention projection
  • Year-end brand positioning and market readiness packet

8. Ad-Hoc Reporting

The CEO may request special or urgent reporting on any customer experience matter, including:

  • Market expansion customer experience analysis
  • Pricing and promotional sensitivity analysis
  • Crisis customer experience modeling
  • Customer data privacy deep-dive review

9. Reporting Accuracy Standards

  • Numbers must reconcile to systems of record
  • All assumptions must be documented
  • Forecasts must have supporting schedules
  • No rounding or simplification without disclosure
  • No unverified customer data allowed

10. Escalation Rules

  • Missed reporting deadlines → immediate CEO escalation
  • Material variance → CEO must be notified same-day
  • Data integrity issues → immediate root-cause analysis
  • Unexpected customer experience incidents → flag within 24 hours
