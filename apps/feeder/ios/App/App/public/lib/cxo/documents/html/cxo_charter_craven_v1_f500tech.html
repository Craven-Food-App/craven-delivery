1. Role Purpose And Strategic Mandate

The Chief Experience Officer of Crave'n is the executive leader responsible for customer experience strategy, brand excellence, customer retention, and revenue growth through exceptional service delivery. The CXO ensures Crave'n operates with clear visibility into customer satisfaction, retention metrics, and experience quality while enabling the company to scale into a market-leading technology-enabled logistics and delivery platform.

The CXO partners with the CEO and executive team to translate Crave'n's mission into a customer-centric business model that drives sustainable growth through superior experience delivery. The CXO is accountable for building and maintaining a customer experience environment that meets or exceeds the expectations of sophisticated customers, strategic partners, and market stakeholders.

2. Strategic Accountability Domains

The CXO is accountable for the enterprise across the following core domains:

2.1 Customer Experience Strategy And Planning

  • Develops and maintains the multi-year customer experience roadmap for Crave'n, including customer journey optimization, retention strategies, and satisfaction targets.
  • Translates strategic objectives into quantifiable customer experience metrics, guardrails, and success criteria.
  • Supports market expansion, pricing, promotions, and product decisions with customer impact analysis.

2.2 Brand And Marketing Excellence

  • Owns the design of brand identity, messaging, and customer communications across all touchpoints.
  • Ensures that all customer-facing systems deliver consistent, high-quality experiences.
  • Implements disciplines that allow Crave'n to build and maintain market-leading brand reputation.

2.3 Customer Performance And Reporting

  • Delivers clear, concise, and reliable customer metrics and experience reports to the CEO and executive team on a defined cadence.
  • Defines and maintains the core performance metrics for customer experience including NPS, CSAT, retention rate, and CAC/LTV.
  • Ensures stakeholders have decision-ready information about customer sentiment and behavior.

2.4 Customer Acquisition And Retention

  • Maintains a forward-looking view of customer acquisition costs, lifetime value, and retention trends.
  • Monitors churn rate and provides an always current view of customer health under multiple scenarios.
  • Establishes customer engagement protocols, loyalty programs, and retention strategies consistent with growth plans.

2.5 Pricing And Promotions Strategy

  • Keeps Crave'n's pricing competitive and aligned with market expectations through well-researched pricing models.
  • Partners with the CEO and CFO on pricing strategy, promotional campaigns, and customer incentive analysis.
  • Coordinates customer research, feedback collection, and responses during strategic planning.

2.6 Customer Data Privacy And Compliance

  • Ensures adherence to applicable customer data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) for all relevant jurisdictions.
  • Implements customer data risk monitoring that surfaces exposure early and clearly.
  • Supports governance processes, including customer data handling and marketing compliance.

2.7 Cross Functional Customer Experience Integration

  • Partners with the COO on delivery experience, driver interactions, and logistics customer impact.
  • Partners with the CTO on platform UX, app experience, and technology-enabled customer touchpoints.
  • Partners with the CFO on pricing, promotions, incentives, and acquisition cost dynamics.

4. Operating Cadence

The CXO is responsible for establishing and maintaining a reliable operating cadence that aligns with Crave'n's growth, customer expectations, and market leadership goals.

4.1 Daily

  • Review customer feedback, support tickets, and satisfaction metrics from previous day.
  • Monitor customer acquisition and retention trends against expectations.
  • Confirm that customer-facing systems are operational and delivering quality experiences.
  • Escalate any material customer experience concern to the CEO without delay.

4.2 Weekly

  • Deliver a concise weekly customer experience snapshot to the CEO including NPS, CSAT, retention rate, and CAC trends.
  • Highlight any deviations from plan or emerging customer sentiment patterns.
  • Align with COO, CTO, and CFO on any customer experience impacts related to their domains.

4.3 Monthly

  • Produce complete monthly customer experience reports and a performance narrative for the CEO.
  • Update the customer journey maps and adjust for new information.
  • Conduct variance analysis versus targets and outline corrective actions where needed.
  • Review compliance status and upcoming customer data privacy or marketing obligations.

4.4 Quarterly

  • Conduct a structured customer experience review with the CEO and executive team.
  • Refine pricing, promotions, and incentive structures in partnership with CFO and COO.
  • Align platform UX and customer touchpoint roadmap with the CTO.
  • Prepare and maintain market-ready customer experience materials and narrative.

5. Cross Functional Collaboration Model

The CXO is expected to operate as a peer level partner with other executives and to provide customer experience leadership that enables, rather than obstructs, execution.

5.1 CEO Partnership

  • Provide the CEO with a continuous and unfiltered view of the company's customer experience position.
  • Support strategic decisions with customer impact assessments, tradeoffs, and risk evaluations.
  • Challenge assumptions constructively when customer experience risk is material.

5.2 COO Partnership

  • Work with the COO on delivery experience, driver-customer interactions, and logistics customer satisfaction.
  • Support logistics design with data on customer experience per order, per market, and per delivery type.

5.3 CTO Partnership

  • Evaluate platform UX and app experience relative to customer expectations and industry standards.
  • Participate in build versus buy assessments for customer-facing technology capabilities.

5.4 CFO Partnership

  • Collaborate on pricing models, promotional spend, incentive structures, and acquisition cost economics.
  • Support promotions and programs with customer lifetime value and payback period analysis.

6. Governance, Controls, And Customer Experience Integrity

The CXO is the primary steward of Crave'n's customer experience integrity and is expected to operate to a standard that would be acceptable in a market-leading technology company.

6.1 Integrity Standards

  • Customer experience reporting must be accurate, supportable, and free from intentional bias.
  • Customer data handling must be documented and consistently applied per privacy regulations.
  • There must be no tolerance for misleading customer communications, hidden fees, or undisclosed terms.

6.2 Control Environment

  • Design and maintain appropriate customer data access controls and privacy safeguards.
  • Implement approval workflows over customer communications and marketing materials.
  • Ensure documentation is sufficient for regulatory review, audit, or customer inquiries.

6.3 Escalation Expectations

  • Immediate escalation to the CEO for material customer experience incidents or reputation risk.
  • Immediate escalation for suspected customer data breaches, privacy violations, or compliance failures.
  • Timely escalation for upcoming regulatory deadlines or potential non-compliance in customer data handling.

7. Performance Measurement

The CXO's performance will be evaluated against qualitative and quantitative criteria that reflect the expectations of a Fortune 500 caliber customer experience leader in a scaling technology company.

7.1 Customer Outcomes

  • Accuracy and predictability of customer satisfaction and retention metrics.
  • Strength and clarity of customer acquisition cost and lifetime value management.
  • Absence of avoidable customer experience surprises or reputation incidents.

7.2 Operational Effectiveness

  • Reliability and timeliness of customer experience reporting cadence.
  • Stability and usability of customer-facing systems and touchpoints.
  • Effectiveness of customer acquisition and retention program support.

7.3 Strategic Impact

  • Quality of customer insight in strategic decision making.
  • Support for market expansion and competitive positioning.
  • Contribution to building a scalable and resilient customer experience model.

7.4 Leadership And Culture

  • Ability to operate with calm, clarity, and customer-centric focus under pressure.
  • Quality of communication with the CEO, executives, and customer-facing teams.
  • Upholding a culture of customer-first thinking, transparency, and accountability within and beyond the CX function.

8. Authority Statement

The CXO holds executive authority over customer experience operations, customer communications, customer data governance, and customer experience risk management, within the strategic parameters established by the CEO and the Board or equivalent governing body. All Crave'n personnel are expected to comply with the frameworks and policies established by the CXO and CEO in relation to customer experience.
