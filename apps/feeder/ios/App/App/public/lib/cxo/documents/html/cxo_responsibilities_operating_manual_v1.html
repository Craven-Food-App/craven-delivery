1. Purpose Of This Manual

This Operating Manual defines the day-to-day, week-to-week, and month-to-month responsibilities of the Chief Experience Officer at Crave'n. It outlines required activities, workflows, review points, escalation paths, and decision-making processes required to ensure customer experience excellence, brand consistency, and operational alignment.

2. Core Responsibilities Overview

The CXO is responsible for oversight of:

  • Customer experience strategy and journey optimization
  • Customer satisfaction and retention management
  • Customer metrics reporting and performance analysis
  • Brand identity and customer communications
  • Customer data privacy, GDPR/CCPA compliance, and regulatory obligations
  • Customer acquisition and retention strategy
  • Cross-functional customer experience alignment
  • Customer-facing systems, accuracy, and data integrity

3. Daily Responsibilities

3.1 Customer Feedback Review

  • Review previous day customer feedback, support tickets, and satisfaction scores
  • Identify trends, issues, or opportunities requiring immediate attention
  • Flag critical customer experience concerns for escalation
  • Update daily customer health dashboard

3.2 Customer Acquisition Monitoring

  • Review customer acquisition metrics and costs
  • Monitor promotional campaign performance
  • Ensure brand consistency across all customer touchpoints

3.3 Interdepartmental Check-In

  • Respond to CEO rapid requests
  • Coordinate with COO on delivery experience and driver-customer interactions
  • Coordinate with CTO on platform UX and app experience issues
  • Coordinate with CFO on pricing, promotions, and acquisition cost impacts

3.4 Customer-Facing System Integrity

  • Verify system uptime for customer-facing platforms
  • Ensure customer communications are delivered accurately
  • Identify any customer experience issues immediately

4. Weekly Responsibilities

4.1 Weekly Customer Experience Packet To CEO

The weekly packet must include:

  • NPS and CSAT scores
  • Customer retention rate
  • Customer acquisition cost trends
  • Top customer feedback themes
  • Variance vs weekly targets
  • Notes on customer experience risks and mitigations

4.2 Customer Journey Review

  • Review customer journey touchpoints by channel
  • Identify friction points or improvement opportunities
  • Evaluate customer communication effectiveness

4.3 Cross-Functional Alignment Sync

  • COO: delivery experience, driver interactions, logistics customer impact
  • CTO: platform UX, app experience, technology-enabled customer touchpoints
  • CFO: pricing models, promotional spend, acquisition cost economics

5. Monthly Responsibilities

5.1 Month-End Customer Experience Review

  • Finalize all customer metrics for the period
  • Complete customer journey analysis
  • Verify accuracy of customer data and privacy compliance
  • Review customer communication audit trail

5.2 Monthly Customer Experience Reports

The CXO must deliver:

  • Customer satisfaction summary (NPS, CSAT)
  • Customer retention analysis
  • Customer acquisition cost and lifetime value report
  • Customer journey performance by touchpoint
  • Variance vs targets & forecast
  • Narrative commentary for the CEO

5.3 Brand And Communication Compliance

  • Assess brand consistency across channels
  • Identify departures from brand guidelines
  • Recommend corrective actions
  • Provide brand discipline guidance

5.4 Compliance & Privacy Checkpoint

  • Customer data privacy obligations (GDPR, CCPA)
  • Marketing compliance and regulatory filings
  • Customer communication audit requirements

6. Quarterly Responsibilities

6.1 Quarterly Strategic Customer Experience Review

  • Long-range customer experience roadmap update
  • Pricing & promotional structure review
  • Customer retention improvement opportunities
  • Market expansion customer experience readiness

6.2 Market Readiness

  • Prepare customer experience positioning materials
  • Refresh customer KPI dashboards
  • Validate customer data privacy compliance posture
  • Ensure customer research readiness

6.3 Risk Assessment Review

  • Customer satisfaction risk exposure
  • Customer data privacy risk
  • Brand reputation risk
  • Customer acquisition cost trajectory

7. Annual Responsibilities

  • Create annual customer experience plan
  • Formalize 3-year customer experience vision
  • Review brand guidelines & customer communication standards
  • Perform customer data privacy risk assessment
  • Re-evaluate customer-facing systems architecture
  • Prepare annual CEO customer experience strategy summary

8. Customer Experience Controls Responsibilities

  • Define customer data access and privacy controls
  • Implement approval workflows for customer communications
  • Maintain brand guideline enforcement
  • Monitor customer data integrity
  • Ensure compliance-ready documentation
  • Review all customer experience policies annually

9. Crisis Response Responsibilities

9.1 Immediate Action Protocol

  • Freeze all customer communications if reputation risk
  • Provide CEO with emergency customer experience summary
  • Reforecast based on crisis scenario
  • Escalate customer data privacy and brand risks

9.2 Communication Protocol

  • CEO receives hourly or daily updates as required
  • Department heads receive customer impact analysis
  • External stakeholders notified only with CEO direction

10. Executive Collaboration Processes

10.1 CXO + CEO

  • Customer experience visibility
  • Strategic decision customer impact assessment
  • Brand & market positioning strategy

10.2 CXO + COO

  • Delivery experience optimization
  • Driver-customer interaction standards
  • Logistics customer satisfaction

10.3 CXO + CTO

  • Platform UX and app experience control
  • Customer-facing technology oversight
  • Customer touchpoint scalability economics

10.4 CXO + CFO

  • CAC & LTV tracking
  • Promotional & incentive financial impact
  • Customer acquisition and retention economics

11. Escalation Expectations

  • Customer satisfaction or retention risks
  • Customer data privacy breaches
  • Brand reputation incidents
  • Marketing compliance violations
  • Miscalculated customer projections
  • System failures impacting customer experience
