## 1. Purpose Of This Framework

This Governance Framework defines how the CXO establishes, operates, and maintains customer experience governance at Crave'n. It ensures customer-centricity, brand consistency, data privacy compliance, and alignment across all customer-facing domains. Designed to match Fortune 500 tech-company standards.

---

## 2. Governance Scope

The CXO's governance authority includes:

- Customer experience strategy & planning
- Brand identity & customer communications
- Customer metrics & performance analytics
- Customer data privacy & GDPR/CCPA compliance
- Marketing compliance & regulatory obligations
- Customer acquisition & retention strategy
- Cross-functional customer experience planning
- Customer-facing systems & data governance

---

## 3. Governance Principles

**Customer-Centricity** – All decisions prioritize customer value and experience quality.

**Brand Consistency** – Brand identity and messaging are reliable and consistent.

**Data Privacy & Compliance** – Customer data handling meets all regulatory requirements.

**Alignment & Integration** – Customer experience aligns with operational realities.

**Scalability** – Customer experience systems scale with growth.

---

## 4. Governance Operating Model

**Strategic Governance:**

- Annual customer experience plan
- Strategic customer experience vision
- Customer acquisition & retention strategy
- Brand positioning governance

**Operational Governance:**

- Customer metrics reporting cadence
- Customer feedback monitoring
- Customer communication governance
- Delegation of authority

**Control Governance:**

- Customer data privacy controls
- System access governance
- Marketing compliance monitoring
- Risk escalation pathways

**Information Governance:**

- Customer data systems ownership
- Customer metrics standards
- Data retention & privacy compliance
- Customer data source-of-truth architecture

---

## 5. Delegation Of Authority And Escalation

Escalation triggers include:

- Customer satisfaction or retention deviations
- Customer data privacy breaches
- Marketing compliance risk
- Reputation incidents
- Any threat to customer experience or market position

Escalation path:

1. CXO to CEO
2. CXO to COO/CTO/CFO (if impacted)
3. CXO to Board (through CEO)

---

## 6. Governance Cadence

**Weekly:**

- Customer experience snapshot
- Customer metrics update
- Customer feedback review

**Monthly:**

- Full customer experience reporting
- Variance analysis
- Compliance checkpoint

**Quarterly:**

- Strategic review
- Pricing & promotions review
- Market readiness review

## 7. Governance Outputs

- Monthly reporting package
- Quarterly strategic review
- Compliance schedule
- Customer experience risk register
- Delegation of authority matrix
