## 1. Role Purpose And Strategic Mandate

The Chief Experience Officer of Crave'n is the executive leader responsible for customer experience strategy, brand excellence, customer retention, and revenue growth through exceptional service delivery. The CXO ensures Crave'n operates with clear visibility into customer satisfaction, retention metrics, and experience quality while enabling the company to scale into a market-leading technology-enabled logistics and delivery platform.

The CXO partners with the CEO and executive team to translate Crave'n's mission into a customer-centric business model that drives sustainable growth through superior experience delivery.

---

## 2. Strategic Accountability Domains

### 2.1 Customer Experience Strategy And Planning

- Develops and maintains the multi-year customer experience roadmap
- Translates strategic objectives into quantifiable customer experience metrics
- Supports market expansion, pricing, promotions, and product decisions with customer impact analysis

### 2.2 Brand And Marketing Excellence

- Owns the design of brand identity, messaging, and customer communications
- Ensures that all customer-facing systems deliver consistent, high-quality experiences
- Implements disciplines that allow Crave'n to build and maintain market-leading brand reputation

### 2.3 Customer Performance And Reporting

- Delivers clear, concise, and reliable customer metrics and experience reports
- Defines and maintains core performance metrics: NPS, CSAT, retention rate, CAC/LTV
- Ensures stakeholders have decision-ready information about customer sentiment and behavior

### 2.4 Customer Acquisition And Retention

- Maintains forward-looking view of customer acquisition costs, lifetime value, and retention trends
- Monitors churn rate and provides current view of customer health
- Establishes customer engagement protocols, loyalty programs, and retention strategies

### 2.5 Pricing And Promotions Strategy

- Keeps Crave'n's pricing competitive and aligned with market expectations
- Partners with CEO and CFO on pricing strategy, promotional campaigns, and customer incentive analysis
- Coordinates customer research, feedback collection, and responses during strategic planning

### 2.6 Customer Data Privacy And Compliance

- Ensures adherence to customer data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA)
- Implements customer data risk monitoring
- Supports governance processes for customer data handling and marketing compliance

### 2.7 Cross Functional Customer Experience Integration

- Partners with COO on delivery experience, driver interactions, and logistics customer impact
- Partners with CTO on platform UX, app experience, and technology-enabled customer touchpoints
- Partners with CFO on pricing, promotions, incentives, and acquisition cost dynamics

## 4. Operating Cadence

### 4.1 Daily

- Review customer feedback, support tickets, and satisfaction metrics
- Monitor customer acquisition and retention trends
- Confirm customer-facing systems are operational
- Escalate material customer experience concerns to CEO

### 4.2 Weekly

- Deliver weekly customer experience snapshot to CEO (NPS, CSAT, retention, CAC trends)
- Highlight deviations from plan or emerging customer sentiment patterns
- Align with COO, CTO, and CFO on customer experience impacts

### 4.3 Monthly

- Produce complete monthly customer experience reports
- Update customer journey maps
- Conduct variance analysis versus targets
- Review compliance status for customer data privacy and marketing obligations

### 4.4 Quarterly

- Conduct structured customer experience review with CEO and executive team
- Refine pricing, promotions, and incentive structures
- Align platform UX roadmap with CTO
- Prepare market-ready customer experience materials

---

## 5. Cross Functional Collaboration Model

### 5.1 CEO Partnership

- Provide continuous and unfiltered view of customer experience position
- Support strategic decisions with customer impact assessments
- Challenge assumptions when customer experience risk is material

### 5.2 COO Partnership

- Work on delivery experience, driver-customer interactions, logistics customer satisfaction
- Support logistics design with customer experience data per order, per market

### 5.3 CTO Partnership

- Evaluate platform UX and app experience relative to customer expectations
- Participate in build versus buy assessments for customer-facing technology

### 5.4 CFO Partnership

- Collaborate on pricing models, promotional spend, incentive structures, acquisition cost economics
- Support promotions with customer lifetime value and payback period analysis

---

## 6. Governance, Controls, And Customer Experience Integrity

### 6.1 Integrity Standards

- Customer experience reporting must be accurate, supportable, and free from bias
- Customer data handling must be documented and consistently applied per privacy regulations
- No tolerance for misleading customer communications, hidden fees, or undisclosed terms

### 6.2 Control Environment

- Design and maintain customer data access controls and privacy safeguards
- Implement approval workflows over customer communications and marketing materials
- Ensure documentation sufficient for regulatory review, audit, or customer inquiries

### 6.3 Escalation Expectations

- Immediate escalation for material customer experience incidents or reputation risk
- Immediate escalation for customer data breaches, privacy violations, or compliance failures
- Timely escalation for regulatory deadlines or potential non-compliance

---

## 7. Performance Measurement

### 7.1 Customer Outcomes

- Accuracy and predictability of customer satisfaction and retention metrics
- Strength of customer acquisition cost and lifetime value management
- Absence of avoidable customer experience surprises

### 7.2 Operational Effectiveness

- Reliability and timeliness of customer experience reporting
- Stability and usability of customer-facing systems
- Effectiveness of customer acquisition and retention programs

### 7.3 Strategic Impact

- Quality of customer insight in strategic decision making
- Support for market expansion and competitive positioning
- Contribution to scalable and resilient customer experience model

### 7.4 Leadership And Culture

- Ability to operate with customer-centric focus under pressure
- Quality of communication with CEO, executives, and customer-facing teams
- Upholding culture of customer-first thinking and accountability

---

## 8. Authority Statement

The CXO holds executive authority over customer experience operations, customer communications, customer data governance, and customer experience risk management, within strategic parameters established by the CEO and Board.
