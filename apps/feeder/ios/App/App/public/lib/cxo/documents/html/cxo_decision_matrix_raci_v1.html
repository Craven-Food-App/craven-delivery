1. Purpose Of This Matrix

This Decision Rights Matrix defines the formal decision-making authority and responsibilities for Crave'n's Chief Experience Officer (CXO), in relation to the CEO, CFO, CTO, and COO. It establishes clear accountability and alignment across strategic, customer experience, brand, and compliance-related areas.

2. RACI Legend

  • D – Decision Authority: Final decision-making power.
  • A – Accountable: Owns the outcome; answerable for final output.
  • R – Responsible: Performs the work or analysis.
  • C – Consulted: Provides input before a decision.
  • I – Informed: Notified after a decision.

4. Rules Of Engagement

4.1 CXO Must Escalate To CEO When:

  • Customer satisfaction or retention deviates materially from targets.
  • Customer data privacy breach or compliance risk surfaces.
  • Reputation incident or brand damage risk appears.
  • A decision has cross-functional customer impact.

4.2 CXO Has Independent Authority Over:

  • Customer communication schedules and content.
  • Customer feedback collection and analysis.
  • Brand messaging and identity guidelines.
  • Customer data privacy controls and procedures.
  • Customer experience measurement and reporting.

5. Revision & Review

This matrix shall be reviewed annually by the CXO and CEO. Updates require CEO approval.
