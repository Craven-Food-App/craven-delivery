SECTION 1 — POLICY PURPOSE

This Executive Discipline & Accountability Policy ("Policy") establishes the standards, escalation procedures, and corrective actions applicable to all C-Suite Officers, Senior Executives, Executive Directors, and Vice Presidents of Crave'n (the "Corporation").

Executives are fiduciaries of the Corporation and are held to strict standards of performance, responsiveness, governance compliance, and operational reliability.

SECTION 2 — CEO DIRECTIVES

All directives issued by the Chief Executive Officer ("CEO") are mandatory. Executives are expected to execute directives promptly, without repeated reminders, delay, or deviation. Non-performance constitutes breach of executive duty.

SECTION 3 — EXECUTIVE ACCOUNTABILITY FRAMEWORK

Executive performance issues are addressed through the following escalating instruments:

  1. Executive Performance Memorandum (EPM)
  2. Executive Corrective Action Plan (ECAP)
  3. Board Notice of Non-Compliance (BNNC)
  4. Termination for Cause Notice (ETFCN)

SECTION 4 — EXPECTATIONS OF CONDUCT

Executives must:

  • Meet deadlines and deliverables issued by the CEO
  • Maintain department operational readiness
  • Ensure portal functionality
  • Ensure cross-department coordination
  • Maintain professional communication
  • Demonstrate proactive leadership

Failure triggers accountability under this Policy.

SECTION 5 — CONSEQUENCES

Executives failing to meet expectations may face:

  • Suspension of bonus eligibility
  • Pause of share vesting
  • Reduction of authority
  • Removal from position
  • Termination for Cause with forfeiture of unvested equity

SECTION 6 — AUTHORITY

The CEO may issue EPM and ECAP instruments. The Board or Executive Committee may issue BNNC and ETFCN instruments.

SECTION 7 — RECORDKEEPING

All documents must be stored in the Executive Portal and mirrored to corporate records.
