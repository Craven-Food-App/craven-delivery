1. PURPOSE OF THIS FRAMEWORK

This framework defines how the CFO establishes, operates, and maintains financial governance at Crave'n. It ensures discipline, accountability, transparency, and alignment across all financial domains. Designed to match Fortune 500 tech-company standards.

2. GOVERNANCE SCOPE

The CFO's governance authority includes:

- Financial strategy and multi-year planning

- Liquidity and treasury operations

- Financial reporting and performance analytics

- Accounting integrity and financial controls

- Compliance with regulatory and tax requirements

- Investor readiness and capital formation

- Cross-functional planning with leadership

- Financial systems and data governance

3. GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

Transparency – Financial reporting is accurate and unfiltered.  

Predictability – Reporting rhythms and forecasts are reliable.  

Control & Accountability – Controls, approvals, and policies are enforced.  

Alignment & Integration – Finance aligns with operations.  

Scalability – Governance must scale with growth.

4. GOVERNANCE OPERATING MODEL

Strategic Governance:

- Annual budgeting

- Strategic financial roadmap

- Capital allocation

- Fundraising governance

Operational Governance:

- Reporting cadence

- Cash forecasting

- Vendor and spend governance

- Delegation of authority

Control Governance:

- Internal controls

- System access governance

- Compliance monitoring

- Risk escalation pathways

Information Governance:

- Financial systems ownership

- Reporting standards

- Data retention and audit readiness

- Financial data source-of-truth architecture

5. DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY AND ESCALATION

Escalation triggers include:

- Cash deviations vs forecast

- Vendor/contract exposure

- Compliance risk

- Fraud or misconduct

- Any threat to operational or financial stability

Escalation path:

1. CFO to CEO

2. CFO to COO/CTO/CXO (if impacted)

3. CFO to Board (through CEO)

6. GOVERNANCE CADENCE

Weekly:

- Finance snapshot

- Cash forecast

- Vendor exposure review

Monthly:

- Financial statements

- Variance analysis

- Compliance checkpoint

Quarterly:

- Strategic review

- Pricing and payout structure review

- Investor readiness review

7. GOVERNANCE OUTPUTS

- Annual financial plan

- Weekly financial summary

- Monthly reporting package

- Quarterly strategic review

- Compliance schedule

- Financial risk register

- Delegation of authority matrix

8. GOVERNANCE RISK DOMAINS

- Liquidity risk

- Financial accuracy risk

- Operational cost exposure

- Pricing and payout risk

- Vendor exposure

- Compliance and regulatory risk

- Investor reporting risk
