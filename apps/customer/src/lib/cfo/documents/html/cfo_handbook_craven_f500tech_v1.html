1. Introduction

The Crave'n CFO Executive Handbook defines the professional, ethical, and behavioral standards expected of the Chief Financial Officer. It outlines the conduct, responsibilities, collaboration rules, and leadership expectations required to operate at a Fortune 500 level within a high-growth technology company.

The CFO plays a central role in Crave'n's long-term success, financial integrity, and operational discipline. This handbook exists to ensure consistent, predictable, and accountable leadership.

2. Executive Conduct Expectations

  • Operate with professionalism, emotional control, and integrity at all times.
  • Communicate clearly, directly, and respectfully with all executives and staff.
  • Maintain confidentiality regarding all strategic, financial, and personnel matters.
  • Treat all employees fairly and consistently.
  • Demonstrate executive maturity: no impulsive decisions, no reactive communication.

3. Ethical And Integrity Standards

  • Uphold zero tolerance for fraud, manipulation, or misrepresentation.
  • Ensure financial reporting is factual, unbiased, and complete.
  • Adhere to all federal, state, and corporate compliance requirements.
  • Reject any requests or actions that compromise financial integrity.
  • Maintain transparent communication with the CEO regarding all material risks.

4. Executive Leadership Expectations

  • Model calm, collected, and strategic thinking under pressure.
  • Solve problems through structured analysis and data-driven decisions.
  • Support cross-functional alignment without creating friction.
  • Hold departments accountable while maintaining respectful collaboration.
  • Lead with clarity, predictability, and accountability.

5. Interaction With The CEO

5.1 Communication Cadence

  • Daily: Liquidity, major risks, urgent updates.
  • Weekly: Full finance packet and short-term runway review.
  • Monthly: Full financial statements and commentary.
  • Quarterly: Strategic and market expansion review.

5.2 Behavioral Expectations

  • Provide unfiltered truth and complete transparency.
  • Escalate material issues immediately—not after investigation.
  • Challenge assumptions respectfully.
  • Never blindside the CEO with unexpected financial outcomes.

5.3 Decision Expectations

  • Present options and tradeoffs—not just problems.
  • Base recommendations on data, not personal preference.
  • Document decisions and maintain audit-ready records.

6. Cross-Functional Collaboration Standards

6.1 With COO

  • Align on delivery economics, cost structure, and operations planning.
  • Collaborate on market readiness evaluations.

6.2 With CTO

  • Monitor cloud and platform cost trajectory.
  • Evaluate vendor decisions from cost and scalability standpoint.

6.3 With CXO

  • Track CAC, LTV, incentives, and promotion economics.
  • Align pricing and fee models with customer impact.

7. Communication Standards

  • Communicate concisely, with financial precision.
  • Avoid overly emotional, reactive, or impulsive language.
  • Provide executives with clear status updates and next steps.
  • Never communicate in a way that undermines trust or alignment.

8. Reporting Discipline

  • Deliver reports on schedule—no exceptions.
  • Ensure forecasts and statements are fully supportable.
  • Do not revise numbers without documentation and CEO awareness.

9. Confidentiality Requirements

  • Protect all financial data, investor materials, and internal reports.
  • Avoid discussing sensitive matters with unauthorized individuals.
  • Do not share screenshots, documents, or spreadsheets outside approved channels.

10. Conflict Of Interest & Independence

  • Disclose any real or potential conflict to the CEO immediately.
  • The CFO must avoid financial entanglements with Crave'n vendors or partners.
  • No use of company resources for personal benefit.

11. Meeting Conduct Standards

  • Arrive prepared with all relevant numbers and documents.
  • Speak with clarity and authority.
  • Do not dominate discussions—enable collaboration.
  • Keep debates professional, analytical, and respectful.

12. Decision-Making Standards

  • Base all recommendations on objective data.
  • Provide CEO with financial implications for each option.
  • Do not advance decisions without cross-functional consultation.

13. Crisis Leadership Expectations

  • Act with calm urgency—never panic.
  • Communicate quickly and accurately.
  • Switch to crisis cadence: hourly/daily reporting.
  • Support CEO in stabilizing the organization.

14. Travel & Expense Responsibilities

  • Expenses must be reasonable, justified, and pre-approved when required.
  • No personal spending or mixed-use charges.
  • All receipts must be submitted timely.
  • Travel must be cost-conscious and aligned with corporate guidelines.

15. Financial Documentation Standards

  • Maintain complete, version-controlled documentation.
  • Ensure all financial statements are archived and timestamped.
  • Use audit-ready structure for all calculations and models.

16. Integrity In Forecasting

  • Forecasts must be realistic—not optimistic guesses.
  • Assumptions must be documented and shared.
  • CEO must be informed of any shift in forecast assumptions immediately.

17. Executive Availability & Responsiveness

  • Respond rapidly to CEO communications.
  • Be available during critical business periods without exception.
  • Maintain predictable and reliable communication.

18. Respect & Conduct Within The Company

  • No disrespectful or dismissive behavior.
  • No condescending language toward staff or executives.
  • Uphold Crave'n's standards of professionalism and character.

19. Non-Fraternization Policy

The CFO must not engage in any romantic, financial, or inappropriate personal relationships with employees, executives, or contractors. This is to prevent conflicts of interest, favoritism, and organizational risk.

20. Performance Evaluation

  • Quarterly performance review with CEO.
  • Annual performance evaluation.
  • Measurement based on accuracy, predictability, leadership, and integrity.

21. CEO & Executive Feedback Loop

  • The CFO must be open to constructive feedback.
  • Act promptly on CEO direction.
  • Demonstrate ongoing improvement in leadership, communication, and discipline.

22. Violations & Consequences

  • Failure to meet reporting deadlines may result in corrective action.
  • Misrepresentation of financial data results in immediate termination.
  • Unprofessional behavior may result in disciplinary review.
  • Non-compliance with ethical standards may trigger removal from role.
