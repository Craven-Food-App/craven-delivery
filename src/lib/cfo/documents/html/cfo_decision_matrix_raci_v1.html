1. Purpose Of This Matrix

This Decision Rights Matrix defines the formal decision-making authority and responsibilities for Crave'n's Chief Financial Officer (CFO), in relation to the CEO, CTO, COO, and CXO. It establishes clear accountability and alignment across strategic, financial, operational, and compliance-related areas.

This matrix follows a Fortune-500 style RACI model (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) combined with Decision Authority (D).

2. RACI Legend

  • D – Decision Authority: Final decision-making power.
  • A – Accountable: Owns the outcome; answerable for final output.
  • R – Responsible: Performs the work or analysis.
  • C – Consulted: Provides input before a decision.
  • I – Informed: Notified after a decision.

4. Rules Of Engagement

4.1 CFO Must Escalate To CEO When:

  • Runway deviates from forecast materially.
  • Cash reserves fall below threshold.
  • A financial control or compliance risk surfaces.
  • Major vendor or contract exposure appears.
  • A decision has cross-functional risk.

4.2 CFO Has Independent Authority Over:

  • Financial reporting schedules.
  • Internal financial controls.
  • Financial systems architecture.
  • Reconciliations & closing procedures.
  • Expense management discipline.

5. Revision & Review

This matrix shall be reviewed annually by the CFO and CEO. Updates require CEO approval.
